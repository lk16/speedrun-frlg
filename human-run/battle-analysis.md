# What a lone starter-line carry can beat: a turn-level battle simulation

`crates/frlg-human/src/battle.rs` simulates a fight turn by turn with the decomp's damage formula,
stats (base, IV, EV, nature), move data, learnsets, type chart and the trainers' actual mons, IVs and
moves (`tools/gen_dex.py` -> `data/dex.json`). `frlg-human battle <TRAINER_ID> <level> [--surf]
[--badges mask] [--league]` runs it; `Params::battle_sim` swaps it into the route simulation
(experimental, **not** the baseline of any plan).

## Assumptions (all of them)

* The carry is the Squirtle line (Wartortle 16, Blastoise 36) with IVs 20, EVs 0, neutral nature; level
  up moves known at its level plus Surf after the Safari trip and Earthquake after Giovanni; badge
  stat boosts (+10%: Boulder attack, Soul defence, Volcano special) when earned; Torrent at 1/3 HP.
* No second mon, no switching, no status or stat-stage moves on either side, no abilities except
  Torrent, no held items, no PP limit.
* The enemy uses its move with the best expected damage; both sides take 85-100 percent random damage,
  crits at 1 in 16, accuracy as listed (`battle_script_commands.c:588,1199`, `pokemon.c` `CalculateBaseDamage`).
* Heal items are used below 40% HP (a turn each): Potion 20 HP to level 14, Super Potion 50 to 29,
  Hyper Potion 200 after, Full Restore (999) in the League.
* 1500 simulated fights per cell, seed 1.

## What it says

* The original model's level-ratio shortcut (`p_lose` centred at a level ratio of 1.25) and its
  type-matchup floor of 0.9 make these fights easy. A lone Wartortle at level 26 (the plan's level at Lt.
  Surge) wins about 5% of the time: Raichu's Thunderbolt (STAB, 2x on Water) outspeeds and out-damages it,
  and a lone carry needs about level 29-30 there. Sabrina (Psychic, 4 mons of 37-43) needs about 42+, and
  the Elite Four at level 54 is 56-99% per member, 68% for the Champion (about 20% for the whole chain
  without a second fighter or better coverage).
* So the plans' single-carry assumption is optimistic exactly where a human would bring a second mon (a
  Ground type for Surge, for instance). The fix is a party model (catch, level and use a counter), not a
  tuning of this plan; running the route simulation with `battle_sim` on gives **no finishes at all** for
  the current plans because of Surge, and adding pre-Surge grinding alone does not fix it (Sabrina and the
  rivals are the next walls).
* The sensitivity table in `journal/2026-11-10-09-40-starters-audit-safari.md` (`type_floor`,
  `team_bonus`, loss-curve centre) is the old model's version of the same uncertainty.

## A counter exists (Surge)

`frlg-human battle TRAINER_LEADER_LT_SURGE <level> --species SPECIES_X --badges 1` (a second fighter with its own
level-up moves, IV 20, no EV, unevolved, no items): Geodude L18 wins 100% in ~10 turns (L22: 6.5 turns), Sandshrew
L18/22 100% (15/11 turns), Diglett L18/22 99.7%/100% (19/14 turns, it only knows Scratch there), Dugtrio 100%. All are
Ground types, immune to every Electric move Surge's team has (`battle_main.c:312`). Geodude and Sandshrew are wild on
the route (LeafGreen: Mt Moon and Route 3/4 tables in `data/dex.json`), Diglett in its cave. So the missing piece for a
robust plan is cheap in principle (catch one, level it a little on the way, switch for the Surge fight); the model
has no party, so it cannot price it. Sabrina (Psychic) has no equally clean counter among Kanto's early mons (Bug and
Ghost are the weaknesses; Gastly/Haunter from the Tower, Pinsir/Scyther in the Safari, Jolteon via a Thunder Stone);
the lone carry needs level 42+ there.
