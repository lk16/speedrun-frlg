- "It's **two frame-perfect manips to even start the run**" (1:34): one decides the starter

  and drifts: "started the stream with +114 ... hit the first two Squirtles and then was late six
  times, so it's 110 now; 4 ms is a quarter of a frame" (1:51). He loses attempts in clusters when it
  drifts ("lost five or six attempts", 1:37; "perfect press two frames late", 7:57; "missed the
  encounter 12 times in a row", 7:50; "one frame off in both directions this many times", 7:49).
  A 114 -> 110 change was enough to flip hit/miss. 2:43 and 7:10: "a quarter of a frame" shift is enough.

  viewer says **v2.4.3** has a good hit rate; he considers switching back, 1:30, 7:57). He wishes for
  a daily offset-calibration test, notes it depends on how the A button is pressed (1:52).

  row" (1:01). UNCLEAR whether 97.71% is the program's chance of landing a runnable starter, or something
