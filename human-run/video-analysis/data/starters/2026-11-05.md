## How the box behaves (differs from the task's assumption)
- The box updates when the starter is rolled, at attempt timer ~0:45-0:58. It is NOT shown "then reset
  immediately": almost every attempt keeps its starter on screen for ~1:45-2:20 of timer (an "OFF SCREEN n /
  ANCHORS a . b . c" panel replaces the IVs around timer 1:42-2:10 and alternates with them), and rejects
  reset at timer ~1:50-2:30. The old starter stays displayed after reset until the next attempt rolls its
  own at ~0:50 (readings at timer 0.0 are the previous attempt's leftovers or title-screen readings).
- Often a second reading replaces the first 10-40 s later in the same attempt (first reading at ~0:50, second at
  ~1:05-1:25); the second is the one the attempt then keeps. First readings are tagged TRANSIENT.
- Because of that, "ACCEPTED" below means: the box stayed unchanged for >~4.5 min / timer evidence >3 min, i.e.
  the attempt ran on past the lab. Timer past ~1:00 is never observed for rejects, so rejects are
  REJECTED_LAB (reset ~1:50-2:35). Intermediate outcomes are kept apart: LATE_RESET_OR_AMBIG (timer 2:00-~4:00 at
  reset or a bounded range), AMBIG_LONG (could be accepted-and-died soon, could be a late reset).
  Timer values past the first screen are only known where a 10 s sample hit that attempt; many outcomes are
  upper-bounded from the next attempt's start time, hence low confidence.
- Outcome is mostly inferred from timer/box persistence, not from watching the game window.
