External-claim findings (what the runner's overlay showed), not decomp-checked.
Data: `2026-11-07.jsonl` (38 distinct readings, 21 attempts). Scratch method: keyframe-only decode
(-discard nokey, ~3233 keyframes, avg gap ~5.6 s, max ~7 s) of the IV box 1340,985 220x72 (layout
as in METHOD.md; confirmed), changed-pixel detection to find every change (96 change events, so no
fixed-interval sampling gap), then zoomed sheets of every change read by eye. A second pass tracked
the big timer's green (running) vs grey (0.0) colour per keyframe to get each attempt's length.
Timestamps are keyframe times, up to ~7 s after the true change.

## What the box shows (per attempt)
- Roughly 45-58 s of timer into an attempt the box (6 IVs, NATURE, FRAME) updates to a reading.
  Often 2-3 readings follow in quick succession (5-30 s apart; FRAME changes each time). The last
  reading before the box flips to `OFF SCREEN n / ANCHORS a . b . c` is the "final" one; earlier
  ones are tagged `pre-lab` in the jsonl (game window at that point: walking in Pallet / Route 1
  area; the final reading appears around the lab/rival "Gramps!" scene, 1488 s checked).
  Whether pre-lab values are predictions or earlier RNG states is not established from video.
- `OFF SCREEN n` (n = 0..4) with three ANCHORS numbers (about 1675-2250 . 2500-3085 . 3800-4480)
  shows at timer ~1:40-2:10 (e.g. 3:28:32, when the game shows "received the SQUIRTLE" at timer
  ~1:42) in almost every attempt, accepted or rejected. Meaning unknown (timing helper).
- After a reset the box shows all zeros / empty NATURE and FRAME; once (2:09:24) it is black.
- At 1:08:00 (attempt R7) a final reading 8133 Serious 27/9/20/12/15/15 appears AFTER an OFF SCREEN
  and after pre-final 8135 Rash; treated as final, medium confidence on which one is "the" starter.
- Box is always readable; no unreadable cells. HP first digit is partly under the burned timestamp
  only in my zoom sheets (values taken from the non-overlapped sheet).

## Outcome rule used
Attempt = one green run of the LiveSplit timer. ACCEPTED if the timer kept running >= 5 min
(all accepted ones: 12-93 min, game confirmed past lab for 8567: rival fight at ~3:28:50, Wartortle
at 4:00, Blaine split in progress at video end 4:59:30, timer 1:32:42). REJECTED if it reset within
84-170 s of timer start (median ~133 s), i.e. shortly after OFF SCREEN. Gap between 170 s and 5 min
is empty, so there is no borderline case. Final reading drives the outcome.
