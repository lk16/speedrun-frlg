Source: `main97-twitch-stream-2026-11-12.mp4` (1920x1080, 8h24m, video time = stream time).
Data: `2026-11-12-twitch.jsonl` (one object per distinct reading appearance, 77 lines; fields `attempt`,

## Method (what was actually done)
- Probed frames: the IV box is at 1340,985 220x72 and is **always on screen**; the big timer is right-aligned at
  ~x 130-355, y 950-1000 (minutes extend left; digits go green while running, grey `0.0` when reset/idle).
- Keyframe-only decode (2 s grid), crop the box to 110x36 gray, segment on pixel change (>=10 px differing by >70)
  -> 488 raw states, 186 outside the BRB/signal-loss spans. One cell per state (box @1.0x + timer at start + timer at end),
  then every state re-read at 1.6x (all natures/digits double-checked), plus timer samples every 20-40 s inside every
  long state to find reset times, plus a chat-column crop 8 s after each state (123 crops).
- Reading is by eye on contact sheets; confidence is "high" for states lasting >= 4 s. 1-2 s states (marked medium) can hide
  neighbouring states: sampling is the 2 s keyframe grid, so a reading shorter than ~2 s would be missed.

## What the box is (observed, not decompiled)
- Per attempt the box **keeps the previous attempt's reading, goes to `0 0 0 0 0 0` when he idles after a reset (timer
  grey 0.0), and shows the new reading at timer ~0:51-0:57 of the new attempt** (right after the new-game intro, bedroom
  scene; seen first at 0:50.7-0:57.3 in all 42 attempts). In the Modest 10386 run the reading is on screen ~55 s before the
  game gives "received the SQUIRTLE" (~1:56). So it is a prediction/preview of the starter, not a read of the party.
- Within one attempt it often changes: 28 of 43 attempts have 2-4 readings (transients 1-30 s long, e.g. 27/6/26/30/14/26 Mild 9431 ->
  15/12/20/31/20/27 Rash 9705 -> 11/8/31/31/26/30 Rash 9642). Four readings inside 6 s at timer 1:18-1:25 in attempt 10.
  The LAST pre-reset reading is the one he plays on / resets on. (Interpretation, flagged: transients are probably the tool
  re-searching frames before the intro dialogue; the box cannot tell us which one the game finally gives.)
- Frame numbers seen: 8110-11457. A second panel `OFF SCREEN n / ANCHORS a . b . c` (n = 0-5; anchors ~1657-1766 .
  2488-2599 . 3787-3912, or 1933-2308 . 2762-3142 . 4084-4447) replaces the IV box for 2-6 s around timer 1:43-2:08 each
  attempt (e.g. while "received the SQUIRTLE"). Looks RNG-related (anchors); meaning not established here.
- A few off-pattern readings (SpA < 30, odd natures) flash at the very end of an attempt: Hardy 17/12/6/14/24/30 9030 (after the 2:49
  reset of attempt 24), Gentle 19/24/1/24/16/23 8460 (timer 2:52, attempt 38), Quiet 27/31/11/30/21/17 8636 (2:50) + Impish
  21/14/8/28/14/25 8639 (2:52) (attempt 39), Gentle 3/5/16/24/24/20 8415 (1 s at 1:01, attempt 40), Lax 15/23/24/20/19/12 10384
  (2:56, mid-run of accepted attempt 27). All have SpA 14-28 where every real reading has SpA 30/31. Maybe the rival's mon or
  the next-frame preview; unresolved.
