# Starter IV box, main97-youtube-stream-2026-11-04.mp4

External claim (what the runner's overlay showed), not a decomp-checked mechanism. Data:
`2026-11-04.jsonl` (one row per distinct box reading; 57 rows).

## Method

- Video 8:00:41, 1920x1080. Box confirmed at x=1340,y=985,220x72 (same overlay as the other 2026 videos).
- Keyframe-only decode of the box at EVERY keyframe (about every 4.3 s, 6724 keyframes, 0:00:00-8:00:05),
  consecutive-frame pixel dedupe into 280 stable segments, one representative per segment read by eye
  (contact sheet, 56 cells/sheet). A reading that was stable over >= 2 keyframes is "high" confidence; 1-keyframe
  flickers are "medium". No cell was unreadable. Readings shorter than ~4 s could still fall between keyframes.
- Attempts (a "run") were found from the big timer's colour (green = running, grey 0.0 = reset), also per keyframe:
  35 attempts. Outcome per attempt: ACCEPTED if the timer ran >= 500 s (shortest accepted: 540 s; longest rejected
  attempt: 180 s, so there is no grey zone), else REJECTED. Spot-checked on game frames at the end of all 27
  short attempts (rival fight / Oak tutorial / party summary, then reset).
- `shown_in_attempt_s` for an attempt's last reading includes the stale tail only up to the reset.

## Overlay behaviour (new vs METHOD.md)

- The box is blank/zero in two stream scenes (1:09:59-1:12:51, 6:31:51-6:33:51); it also periodically swaps to an
  "OFF SCREEN n / ANCHORS a . b . c" panel (n = 0-4, anchors around 1660-1850 / 2490-2690 / 3800-4000, or
  2170-2270 / 3000-3100 / 4300-4400), and once to "HIT 0% +58 ms / OFF SCREEN 1" at 2:13:11. These are not starters.
- 5:53:35-5:57:29 "Be right back" card, 7:53:05-end "Ending" card (box area shows artwork).
- Each attempt: the box shows the PREVIOUS attempt's stale starter until the timer is ~41-51 s in, then updates to a new
  reading. Often 1-2 flicker readings (1 keyframe) come first, then the stable one at ~+59 s. In 7 attempts a LATE update
  appears 115-177 s in (row role `late_update`), just before the reset: frame number moves by about 1-2 and the starter
  becomes a junk one (SpA 6-27, natures Careful/Modest/Lonely/Naive/Bold/Hardy/Gentle). In the 4 cases where the party
  summary page is on video at the reset (0:52:05 box 8145 Rash -> summary BRAVE; 0:56:05 box 9922 Rash -> NAIVE;
  1:53:59 box 11230 Rash then 11228 Naive -> NAIVE; 2:14:23 box 8600 Rash then 8610 Bold -> BOLD) the in-game nature
  does NOT match the early box reading, and where the box has updated it matches the late reading. Reading: the early
  value is a predicted/target starter, the late one the real one; the runner resets when they disagree. Not seen for
  accepted attempts (their summary page is not sampled), so "accepted = prediction hit" is unverified.
