# Main97 stream 2026-11-10: starter IV box readings

Source: `main97-youtube-stream-2026-11-10.mp4` (8 h 24 min, 1920x1080, Main97 overlay layout as in METHOD.md). Raw data: `2026-11-10.jsonl` in this directory (one object per distinct on-screen reading; 77 readings, 70 distinct seed frames).
External claim from video evidence only; no `decompiled/` citation, so what the box *means* is not established here.

## How it was read

- Box crop 1340,985 220x72 confirmed in the first probe: six IV columns (HP ATK DEF SPA SPD SPE), `NATURE`, `FRAME`. It also has two other states: all zeros with blank nature/frame (between attempts), and an `OFF SCREEN n / ANCHORS a . b . c` view (alternates with the starter view; not recorded).
- All decodable keyframes (-discard nokey, ~5000 keyframes, every 2-6 s) were cropped; box changes detected by pixel diff (166 + 151 changes), then every distinct change read by eye from contact sheets (cells 330x108, all digits clear; IV/nature/frame confidence high).
- The big timer (crop 225,952 130x48) was classified per keyframe (0.0 / under 1 min / over 1 min) to get each attempt's start, reset time and peak timer (+-6 s), and read by eye at ~480 sampled cells.
- Gaps: decode of keyframes is not continuous in time (a starter shown < ~2-6 s between keyframes could be missed; 1 fps spot check of 3800-4100 s found nothing the keyframes missed). 18508-18964 s and 29908+ s are break/ending cards (no game), 26362 s the box was briefly black.

## What the box is and when it changes (observed)

- A run (timer starts at New Game) shows the box update at timer ~0:36-1:00 (during the intro/before Oak's lab), with fast flicker of several candidate starters over ~2-30 s (frame numbers change, e.g. 8443 then 9160; 9431, 9705, 9431, 9642), settling by about the lab (timer ~1:30). Oak's lab scenes at timer 1:30-1:50; the rival fight at ~2:10-2:30.
- The last reading before ~1:00-1:30 on the timer is taken as the starter of that attempt. Earlier/other readings are marked SUPERSEDED (45 s or less on screen except where the box simply sat unchanged while he was between attempts, e.g. 768 s for Gentle 8460 across a stream pause).
- Several attempts reuse the box value of the previous attempt without the box updating (reset before the update, timer < 1:00 at runs 3351, 3671 s; Modest 11037 reused for a 618 s attempt starting 29246 s).

## Outcome rule (inferred from the timer)

Attempt peak timers are bimodal: 34 attempts peak at 0:44-3:24 (median 2:11) and 11 attempts at 8:54-2:35:57. So:

- ACCEPTED = attempt peak timer >= 5 min (run goes through lab, rival fight, Route 1 and on). 
- REJECTED = reset during lab/rival fight (peak 1:42-3:24), or 2 s after the box showed the starter (1:04:11 Mild 8416, the only "immediate" reject seen).
- Confidence on outcome: medium-high (timer-derived, +-6 s; no per-attempt scene check beyond spot probes at 2825-2893 s lab and 5330-5404 s rival fight).
- NB: nearly all rejects are NOT instant: he plays the lab and the rival fight and resets there (~2:00-2:50). Rival fight / lab is where the decision is evidently made, so the IV box is NOT what he decides on in most cases: IV sets of rejected and accepted overlap completely.
