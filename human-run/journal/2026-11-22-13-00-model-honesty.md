## Battle-bridge bugs

1. **S.S. Anne rival (the big one).** Emulator, 24 seeds from the real s11 state (Wartortle **L27**, stats
   48/53/46/52/47, 76 HP, 62 HP at the start, 4 Potions): **10/24 won** (`mdlsp`, `battle-test`; the turn-1
   outlook said 45%). The sim's own engine at exactly those stats (`frlg-human battle
   TRAINER_RIVAL_SS_ANNE_BULBASAUR 27 --nature 19 --ivs 24,31,20,31,31,20 --evs 16,20,0,20,0,0`, 20k fights):
   36.6% with 4 Potions, 59% with 6 Super Potions, **93% at power 2.0**. So the engine is right and the
   stand-in is wrong: `fight_tactics.rival-ss-anne.risk_power` 2.0 (a 2026-11-17 stipulation for "runner
   skill") doubled Atk/SpA in the lane that decides survival. Lane death there went 3.9% -> 33.5% (sim carry
   L25, Super Potions) with power 1.0. MODEL CHANGE: power 1.0 (+33.8 h [30.3, 37.9] alone). Note the sim
   carry is L25 at this fight, the played s11 run L27.
   Same class, not changed: `rival-cerulean` 3.5 (sim engine L20 with Water Pulse/Mega Kick: 44% at 1.0,
   100% at 3.5; what-if +48.9 h; needs a state at the plan party). Misty 2.0 is harmless (94% at 1.0 and 2.0;
   8/8 more seeds won in the emulator, turn-1 outlook 88%).
2. **No next-mon rule.** Equivalent here: in all 14 emulator losses the lead fainted first and the helpers
   (Paras L6, Weedle L5, Pidgey L3) never won anything. With the plan's party a fighter faint is a loss
   before the League. Not equivalent in the League (Blastoise behind Mewtwo): open.
3. **Potion on a sleeping fighter.** The item turn does not tick the sleep counter (`battle_util.c`
   `CANCELLER_ASLEEP` runs only on a move attempt). `sleep_no_potion`: no potion while asleep unless it
   outheals the foe's best hit (the strict version raised the lane death with Super Potions, 3.9 -> 7.1%: an
   alternating 50-HP potion against Razor Leaf does gain HP). Emulator: same 10/24, same seeds. Sim: no
   effect alone (0.000 h; binds only with Potions). Same code path for the controller (`decide`). Test
   `sleeping_fighter_wakes_or_attacks_instead_of_a_losing_potion`.
4. **Potion tier.** `potion_tiers`: potion HP = level tier capped by the best tier sold on the route so far
   (Potion until the Cerulean mart stop, Super Potion until Cinnabar, then Hyper). +11.4 h [9.4, 13.9]. The
   WR's own purchase (Potions only until Celadon) as a what-if is +410 h: the model is the availability, the
   purchase is the plan's choice.
5. **Balls before Cerulean.** The sim bought up to 20 Poke Balls on demand at the first wanted encounter
   (forest), i.e. about where the Viridian mart is: no real bug. `ball_shop_from`/`early_balls` make the
   purchase explicit at Viridian (3, the WR's stock): +0.39 h [-0.31, 1.08] (R 3.2M, n.s.). Paying 20 balls
   at Viridian for every attempt costs the potion money (53 h at R 3000), and 0 balls is better under the
   honest model (-2.1 h). The s11 shortage was the controller's route script; the plan's `ball: great`
   applies only from Lavender (`max_tier`).
6. **Battle style.** The sim never had the prompt: its battle constants are fitted to the WR (SET). The
   ledger's `04-options` (197 f) sets text speed and scene only; SET is a few more inputs (not modelled).
   The controller must set SET in the opening (it now does at Lavender).
