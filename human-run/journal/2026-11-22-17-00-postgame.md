## Which numbers come from poked runs

Every played time in `calibration.md`'s post-game table and every League-2 rollback count below comes from
**development runs** (`runs/human-play/pg-dev2` .. `pg-dev12`) whose Pokédex was poked to 60 in RAM at the
National Dex step (`FRLG_PG_DEV_DEX`). The poke only touches the dex flags; the legs after it are played inputs,
but those runs are not runs. The poke is refused (Fatal) unless the output directory is named `dev-*` / `*-dev*`,
and the canonical runs (`pg-canon*`, from the v2 Hall of Fame) are started without it: their dex fill is real
hunting (no `DEV:` event in their `events.jsonl`).
