## Decomposing R3c's +3000 h

`FRLG_CATCH_TRACE` (new, diagnostic only) prints every catch attempt and Cut hunt. With `cut_honest` + `cut_rehunt`
on 1930 (seed 8401, R 20k-40k):

* **27 % of the attempts reach the Route 25 hunt with 0 Poke Balls.** The forest catches take the 5 starting balls
  (Caterpie, Weedle, Metapod, Kakuna and Pikachu all have a catch rate of 120 or more, so `pass_reserve` never
  applies). There is no ball stop before Cerulean, and the Cerulean `persim-and-potions` stop is broke: the purse
  is 931 before it, and the segment spends 750 on 3 Awakenings (`awakenings`, 250 each), leaving 181. So the
  controller's "3 Poke Balls at persim-and-potions" (`r3d-ctl`) buys nothing in the model, and the row equals
  `r3d-cut-se`.
* **The old model then walks the whole 300-step budget with an empty bag.** It flees every encounter, Bellsprout
  included, and takes 452 s on average (p10/50/90 408/451/496). Almost all of those attempts reset at
  `bill-cottage` (429 vs 17 resets in the report). That is the "failing the whole attempt" artefact. A human with
  no ball stops and buys some. New MODEL key **`hunt_stop_empty`**: with scheduled marts, a hunt ends when the bag
  is empty. The `cut_rehunt` fallback then tops the bag up to 5 at Cerulean and hunts Route 24 on the bike (78 s).
  Row 1951: **-471 h [-929, -133]** (seed 8402, R 3.2M). Screen: +2997 goes to +2295.
* **The hunt itself costs 73 s when a ball is in the bag** (p10/50/90 21/56/151). The reason is the site: Bellsprout
  is only 25 % of `sRoute25_LeafGreen` (slots 3/4/7, `decompiled/src/data/wild_encounters.json`). Every other
  encounter is an intro plus a flee (450 + 500 f, `calib_enc`), and on Route 25 those take ~3 of every 4.
  The HM01 teach adds 700 f (a guess).
* **Money is not the binding constraint.** The model's prize money checks out against the decomp:
  `4 * lastMonLevel * classMoney` (`decompiled/src/battle_script_commands.c:5373`, `gTrainerMoneyTable` in
  `battle_main.c:451-482`). Brock (Leader 25, Onix L14) gives +1400 and Liam (Camper 5, L11) +220, both matching
  the report. Ball 200, Potion 300, Repel 350 and Nugget 10000 (`items.json`) also match. The purse is 1808 after
  the 10 Pewter Potions and 931 at Cerulean (Magikarp 500, three Mt. Moon Repels 1050), then 4200-5600 after
  Nugget Bridge. Topping up to 2 balls at Pewter costs at most 400 and is cheap: the Route 25 hunt with it is
  +2027 vs +2295 (screen).
* The EHT slope is about 15-17 h per second of mean time near the WR+2m edge: 175 s of extra mean time cost +3000 h,
  and 48 s cost +675 h.

So the cost has two parts. About 1/4 is the dead branch, which was a model artefact and is fixed by
`hunt_stop_empty`. About 3/4 is the hunt's encounter time at a 25 % site. Ball money and Potion money are minor.
