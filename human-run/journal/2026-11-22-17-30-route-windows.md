## trainer_fit 0.6 vs the emulator's 0.95-1.02

Both are right about different things:

1. **The 0.95-1.02 is a pooled number.** Joining the played s11 trainer episodes (`cal_decompose` episodes, `play-battles`
   parties, the events' turn lines; scratch `win-tfit.py`) gives 79 fights: pooled fit 0.95 at the played turns, but
   **one-shot fights (turns <= mons, n 51) 0.85 and multi-turn fights (n 28) 1.12**. `calib_wr`'s 720 + 345 t + 630 m comes
   from 16 long WR fights where t and m are collinear; the played episodes fit **a 838 + t 579 + m 223** (rms 450 f; route
   trainers only: 940 + 433 t + 332 m). A one-shot route trainer is overpriced by the 630 per mon, which a scalar
   `trainer_fit` on intro + mon terms stands in for. MODEL CHANGE `trainer_cost` = [838, 579, 223] replaces the scalar.
2. **The fight list fights more trainers than the WR in Bill and Rock Tunnel** (7 vs 4 on Route 25, 16 vs 10 on Route 9 /
   Rock Tunnel, by the video). With 0.6 the list's extra ~9 fights fit in the WR's windows only because each is priced at
   ~60% of a played fight.
3. **The WR is a bit faster per fight than the bot**: Picnicker Alicia (4 mons, 4 actions) WR 56 s sighting to field, played
   62 s, `trainer_cost` 68 s, `calib_wr` at 1.0 77 s, at 0.6 57 s. Bug Catcher Conner WR ~55 s incl. a level-up, played 53 s.
4. **But the WR's exp comes later.** With the WR's lists in the two windows the carry is 2 levels short from Rock Tunnel to
   Blaine (L31 at Rock Tunnel = WR, but 34 vs 36 at Giovanni 1, 39 vs 41 at Koga): the WR fights the Game Corner door grunt
   and 5 hideout grunts, and uses **two Rare Candies before Giovanni 1** (Lv33 -> 35, "A was elevated to Lv. 35" at 3804,
   evolving at 3814), and the no-reset finishes drop to a third (deaths). So the list must move exp from Route 25 / Rock
   Tunnel to the hideout (and candies) together with the fit, and the risk windows refit after that: not done here.
