# 2026-12-03: evidence-weighted EHT interval for 1057 (interim), evidence readings

Tools (new): `tools/rb_lib.py` (patch/eval/report helpers), `rb_post.py` (runner posteriors Q, D), `rb_mc.py` (Latin-hypercube design, quadratic surface fit, interval), `rb_var.py` (variant x corner grid, written, NOT yet run). Data: `data/robust-mc-1057-pre.json` (96 draws, R 500k per rung, seeds 100+i), `data/robust-cal-1057.json` (Q(cp,lane) and engine-only D(league_power,mewtwo_power) calibration, WR+160m reports).

## 1. Method
Each draw sets: runner pre-door survival Q (posterior of the 63 attempts past the starter gate, late windows pooled k=6: median .060, 95% [.024,.123]; model .0587) -> `lane` scale on all risk-lane powers via the calibration surface; door-to-finish survival D (journal 12-02 posterior Beta(2.2,7.4): median .21, 95% [.04,.53]; `--flatD` data-only Beta(2.5,3.5), median .41) -> `flat` scale on the flat post-door rows given the engine-only survival at the drawn league_power / mewtwo_power; plus league_power N(1.35,.15), mewtwo_power N(1.2,.1), carry_power N(2.0,.2)[1.7,2.4], trainer_fit N(.729,.066), mistake_rate lognormal(.162; 95% [.07,.37]), win_cv U[0,.069], slow_scale lognormal(1,.35), p_hit N(.64,.02), PP Up pickup frames lognormal(450,.35), Elixir/Max pickups x lognormal(1,.3), Aerodactyl/museum special x lognormal(1,.25). Quadratic surface in ln EHT (R2 .98, 5-fold CV R2 .95, residual sd .10 ln incl. sim noise) propagated through 100k prior draws.
Key correction: the "model door-to-finish .155" of journal 12-02 is the WR+2m number (pace resets inside the League); the survival-only comparable (lenient rung WR+160m) is **.244**, i.e. the flat rows already sit at the posterior (.21), not above it. Door-to-finish is 2 finishes of 5 fighters; the pace-gated 11-12 attempt fought nothing.
