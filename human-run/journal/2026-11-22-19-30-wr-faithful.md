Per-event and per-species costs match. WR Safari: gate 4874 -> first encounter 4944 -> out ~5300, 7 species (Doduo?,
Venonat 22, Nidorina 30, Exeggcute 27, Rhyhorn 26, Nidoran F 30, ...; ~11 encounters, 25-35 s per catch, ~10 s per flee),

hazard) vs the WR's ~70 s. Encounter search is cheap and right: LG Safari land rate 21
(`wild_encounters.json`), cooldown `8 - 21/10` = 6 steps (`wild_encounter.c:673-699`), ~10 rolls per encounter at 8.2 f.
(Not modelled: the `encounterRateBuff` growth, +1/200 of the rate per failed roll, `wild_encounter.c:314,374` -- small.)
The difference is the **plan**: the WR hunts the North (Rhyhorn / Nidoran F 20 % slots, Exeggcute 20 %, Nidorina 10 %)
and catches 7, the plan hunts East + West with `safari_stop` 5 and 90/100 steps and gets 4.7; the 2.3 missing species are
the Koga window's -96..-130 s. Power Plant: the WR catches **3**, not 2 (Voltorb "data was added" 5634, Magnemite 5674,
Pikachu 5774; ~180 s), the model 3.2 in 175 s: matches. The predecessor's "~2x" compared the marginal (rare-slot) species.

## Verdict

* **1280 is the honest model and scores worse (+682 h vs 1270).** Every constant in it is measured or read off the video:
  the fight list is the WR's, the per-fight cost is the played run's `trainer_cost` (no 0.6), the carry has the WR's
  level at every split from Brock to Giovanni 3, the windows are refit. 0.6 was hiding ~9 extra Route 25 / Rock Tunnel
  fights and two Cerulean gym trainers; with the WR's list the fight-only windows fit (Bill +14, Rock Tunnel +20,
  Giovanni 1 +29) at the played price. Recommended as model of record; the main session decides CURRENT.
* **No candidate passes the per-window check.** On the WR's structure (1281) the remaining misfits are Snorlax +76
  (tower/Surge fights at the played price), Blaine +73 / Sevii -77 (the model catches one species more before Blaine and one
  fewer on Sevii), Start E4R1 +103 (the end fill), with a total of +319 s: the bot-priced fights are ~7 % slower than the
  WR's. A 0.9 WR-skill factor on `trainer_cost` (1282) brings the total to -20 s but moves other windows out; it rests on two
  WR fights (Alicia 56 vs 62 s, Conner ~1.0), so it is reported as a FIT, not adopted. Note the score sensitivity: x0.9 on
  the fight cost is -907 h on 1280.
* **League levels (1284) are a finding for the League agent**: at the WR's League level (Lv51 at Indigo, 57 at the
  Champion vs the WR's 56) the model's finishes fall from 171 to 27. The League lethality (indigo window 2.0, fatal
  table) is fitted on a carry that ate 4-5 held candies at Indigo, which the WR does not do.
* The WR structure (1281) as a plan is +3266 h vs 1280 under this model: Route 3/4 catches, Safari North with stop 7, the
  late Warden cost exp-free time the model does not win back in the fill (E4R1 window still +103 s).
