## 1. Sacrifices: what the engine did, what the game does

* Engine (`battle.rs`, `Action::Sac`): the foe's move "falls on the sacrifice" **and the fighter attacks (or uses an X item) that same turn from behind it**. In the game the switch to the helper *is* the player's action
  (items and moves act on the active mon); after the helper faints the send-out of the fighter is free but takes no action; the foe's next move is still aimed at the fighter. A sacrifice therefore absorbs one hit and
  spends one turn, and its only value is re-rolling which move the foe picks. Brock at 10/32 HP, no potions, 2 helpers, `frlg-human battle`: no helpers .37, free action 1.00, honest .38.
* Fix: `Tactics::sac_free`, `Params::sac_honest` (outside the League; League sacs keep the older rule because plan 372's WR-calibration used it: **champion 4, champion-2 3, lorelei-2 1 are unvalidated**). Test `honest_sacrifices_give_no_free_action`.
* Helpers: `early_helpers` already limits sacs to the simulated caught bodies minus the starter (`sim.rs` fodder), a dead helper stays dead (no exp is lost: a fainted mon takes no share, so no dilution), 900 frames per sacrifice (guess, unchanged).
  Not modelled: a helper that survives the hit and has to be switched out again (costs another foe hit), the helper's real level/HP vs Onix's Rock Tomb.
* Stream evidence: no source has him send a weak mon first at Brock or at the rivals. The bugs are kept as dex currency ("one bug is enough: evolve it on Koga/revive", T10 2:15-2:41; STRATEGY s4). The only swaps are Spearow
  swapped in to tank Vine Whip at the Cerulean rival (T06 2:07:19-2:08:49, a real attacker, not a sacrifice) and Farfetch'd as Gastly fodder in the Tower (WR 1:10, wild fights). Brock/Route 22/S.S. Anne/Tower-rival sacrifices: **no evidence**.
