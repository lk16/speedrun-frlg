## New primitives

- **Mansion switch solver** (`mansion_to`): door tiles per switch state parsed from
  `data/scripts/pokemon_mansion.inc` (`PressSwitch_*` / `ResetSwitch_*`), applied to other floors as
  planner patches (`Opts::patch`); Dijkstra over (statue, switch state), first statue of the
  cheapest chain pressed, then everything replanned. The yes/no is answered explicitly and the text
  closed with B (a mashed A re-pressed the statue after `releaseall`: a double toggle).
- **Silph card-key doors** (`silph_to`): barriers parsed from `data/scripts/silphco_doors.inc`
  (`EventScript_Close<F>Door<N>`), closed while the door flag is clear; plan with doors shut, else
  as if open, walk to the first closed door on that path and open it.
- **Victory Road boulders** (`strength_to`, `boulder_plan`): BFS over (player, boulders) to put a
  boulder on the `FloorSwitch` coord event of the first closed rock barrier on the path.
- **Planner** (`walk.rs`): spin tiles (0x54-0x58 slide model), warp pads only fire when stepped
  onto (not from the arrival tile), named `LOCALID_*` = object index + 1
  (`tools/mapjson/mapjson.cpp:416`; they were all 0 before, so live positions of named objects were
  ignored), tile **elevation** (`MAPGRID_ELEVATION_MASK`; Victory Road's raised floors), `strict`
  mode (fail fast when live objects wall the way), `plan_len` / `plan_nodes` for dry plans.
- **TM teach with a full move set**: the forget screen gets the sim's `replace_slot` (forced);
  after learning, B instead of A (an A re-selected the TM: "already knows" loop).
- `names.rs` parses `vars.h` (`VAR_*`).
- `shop.rs` (dex agent's file, small fixes): no A-mash into the buy list (an A on the shop menu's
  first frame picked BUY and the following A's bought the cursor's item: ¥40000 of X items, stones
  twice), A only while "Here you go" waits, Cut on the walk to a clerk / PC, storage-menu A retried.
  `dex.rs`: hunts surf on the way when a party mon knows Surf (Route 23's grass).

## Model bugs and model-vs-actual (running list)

1. **No Strength carrier in the plan.** The carry's four slots are all `keep_moves` (Surf, Water
   Pulse, Blizzard, Bite) and no other party mon learns HM04 (`tmhm_learnsets.h`: Pidgey, Weedle,
   Paras, Eevee, Doduo do not). `warden-strength` (1400 frames) teaches Strength to nobody. Played:
   Lapras (Silph 7F gift, to the PC with a full party) withdrawn at the Viridian center for Doduo
   and taught HM04 there: a PC visit (~1200 frames) the model does not have.
2. **No heal between the Mansion and Blaine**, and **none between Blaine and Sabrina's badge**
   (the Sevii fights + hunts): the first branch into Silph blacked out to Scientist Beau. Played:
   Cinnabar center before the gym, Saffron center before Silph.
3. **`stones` sits before the stone purchase.** The model evolves with stones right after Sevii,
   but the plan buys them (`evolutions` `buy: true`) at the Celadon store on fly-celadon-2, later.
4. **TM13 Ice Beam** (`tms` after fly-celadon-2, cost 80000) is a Game Corner prize; this run has no
   Coin Case: not bought (same root as model bug 5 of the previous journal, Abra).
5. **The Silph rival is not forced** on the path the planner takes to 11F (logged when his 7F
   trigger is not crossed); the model counts `rival-silph` as a fight.
6. **Viridian Gym: a beaten trainer can wall the way.** Takashi (10, 2) walks to (10, 4) to battle
   and stays there; the only way from that spin pocket to Giovanni is past him. Played: out of the
   gym and back in (objects reload). Not in the model (`viridian-gym-walk` is 55 tiles).
7. **Balls.** The bag had no Poké Balls after the Safari (the Power Plant and Bond Bridge hunts
   weakened every new species and then fled: "no ball in the bag"). The model's ball money
   (`ball_batch`, `money_model`) assumes a stock bought somewhere before those hunts; the first buy
   on this route is Celadon on fly-celadon-2.
8. **Fly to Route 10** would land 20 rows from the Power Plant's door, but its fly flag is not set
   in this save (the Rock Tunnel leg never entered the Route 10 center): flew to Lavender as the model.
9. **Power Plant hunt** cost 486 s in the canonical pass (200-step budget, no balls); the model's
   `hunt-power_plant-land` is a 200-step wander at walk pace plus encounters.
10. **League heals: Max Potions cannot cure.** The model tops the bag up to `league_heals` 14 at the
    Max Potion price, but its battle policy picks `FullRestore` for a status. With Max Potions every
    branch reached Lance paralysed or poisoned and lost (8 of 8 from the Agatha checkpoint); the
    controller also hung once using a Max Potion on a frozen carry at full HP (bag left open:
    fixed, it attacks instead). Played: 12 Full Restores (3000 each).
11. **PP across the League.** Blastoise reached Lance with Blizzard 3 PP, Surf 9: 10 of 10
    branches won most of Lance and then ran dry. The plan's Elixir (Mansion, `elixirs`) is a
    pickup the model never spends between rooms. Played (`league_prep`): Elixir before a room when
    a move is at <= 5 PP, Full Restore when below half HP or statused.
12. **Champion**: 3 of 10 branches from the Lance checkpoint blacked out to the rival in the first
    E4 pass (before the HoF detection fix the others reached the credits).
13. Victory Road puzzle state resets whenever Route 23 loads (`Route23/scripts.inc` `OnTransition`
    clears every switch var): a hunt on Route 23 between floors would redo the puzzles.
