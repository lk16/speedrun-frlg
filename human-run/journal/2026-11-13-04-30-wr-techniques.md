## Model caveats carried forward

* Fight lengths (20-35 turns at the E4) look ~2× real runs'; enemy stall-move spam follows
  the ported AI scripts' tie-breaking, which is faithful in structure but unverified in
  frequency. Tier-1 calibration of one real E4 fight is the single highest-value check.
* Bulbasaur/Charmander and FireRed numbers above are transplant tests, not fair
  comparisons.
* PP is tracked for the carry only; Mewtwo's rematch chain is exempt (noted in code).

## Corrections (later the same day)

* **Mewtwo cannot learn HM03 Surf** (`tmhm_learnsets.h`: HM04/05/06 only). Every plan from
  303 to 347 taught it, which flattered the rematch Champion fight. The engine now refuses
  illegal teaches and enforces single-use TMs / once-only tutors. Re-scored: 347's 16.08 h
  falls to 9 finishes in 160 000. The legal fix is TM03 Water Pulse, Misty's gift, on Mewtwo
  (plan `348-mewtwo-water-pulse`, **20.06 h**). The numbers above that mention 19.9 h or
  16.08 h are from the flawed plans. Their *paired* verdicts (the WR technique table) were
  measured on plans carrying the same flaw on both sides, so they are probably directionally
  right, but treat them as provisional until re-run on 348+.
* **FireRed vs LeafGreen:** the FR chain's re-descent beat the LG chain by 3.8 h. Flipping only
  the version on the same plan showed the gain came from tactics, not the version. On the
  corrected plan the version difference is not significant (FR − LG = +0.10 h [−0.12, 0.34]).
  In this model the version is a wash.
* **Charmander** is not viable as a lone carry under the exact engine: Brock wins only 1-21%
  at levels 12-14, even with Metal Claw, because Onix's Rock Tomb outruns it. A Charmander plan
  needs a second fighter for Brock, which this single-carry model does not have.
  **Bulbasaur** beats Brock 100% of the time, but cannot learn Dig, Blizzard, Surf or Mega
  Punch. A fair test needs its own TM set (Giga Drain from Erika, Earthquake) and its own
  descent, and has not been run yet.
