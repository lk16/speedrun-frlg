## 1. The 20-death Lance (v3 round 1): a controller bug, not a hazard

From the v3 `agatha` checkpoint, the v3 code lost Lance **30 of 30** times. Every branch had the same shape:
X Accuracy, X Special x2, two Full Restores while setting up, Blizzard on the dragons, then "heal chosen but no heal
item in the bag" with **3 Full Restores still in the bag**, and Blastoise fainted.

Cause: `battle_ram::bag_pocket` stopped at the first empty slot (`take_while(id != 0)`). An item stack used up in
battle leaves its slot empty (`RemoveBagItem`, `decompiled/src/item.c:288-289`) until the bag is opened again and
compacts the pocket (`Pocket_CalculateNItemsAndMaxShowed` -> `BagPocketCompaction`, `item_menu.c:993-997`). After
the second X Accuracy, every item behind that slot (Max Elixir, Full Restores) was invisible: the policy's heal stock
went to 0 and each `Heal` / `FullRestore` turned into an attack. The same bug hit every fight that used up a stack
(X items, potions, the ball pocket), from the first played battle on.

Fixes (3359d289): skip holes (the compacted order is the menu's row order); a plain `Heal` falls back to Max Potion /
Full Restore (`battle::heal_order`; v3 looked only at the potion tiers); potions that heal less than ~1/5 of the bar
(a 20 HP Potion at Lv 58) are not counted as heals. Same state, one try per seed: **7 of 12** Lance wins (seeds 21-32).
`calibration.md` has the table.
