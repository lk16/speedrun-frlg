## 1. Repel timing (Rock Tunnel) - fixed

Cause: `repel_for` ran once per map or trainer approach, and only the dest branch of `travel_to` had the "walk to
where the Repel ends" waypoint (`VAR_REPEL_STEP_COUNT` counts down on every step, `decompiled/src/wild_encounter.c:576-598`;
Repel 100 / Super 200 / Max 250 steps, `decompiled/src/data/items.json` `holdEffectParam`). The trainer branch (the Rock
Tunnel is ten trainers) walked up to a trainer with the Repel lapsing on the way. The tunnel also stocked 3 Repels for
~470 steps.

Fix (`early2.rs`): `repel_waypoint` (shared by both branches): walk to `nodes[left]` when encounter tiles lie beyond it, then
the loop re-uses; Rock Tunnel stock 5 Repels (money-limited by `stock_up`).

Replay, r2j-s31 `bike` -> `rock-tunnel` (plan 1952): wild encounters in the stretch **0** (before: Geodude caught, 2 Zubat
flees); checkpoint frame 239588 -> 237870 (**-1718 f, -29 s**; two extra Repel buys/uses are inside it). 0 bumps.
r2i-s11 `bike` -> `rock-tunnel` (plan 1842) finished after 5 rollbacks (blackouts to Hiker Allen / Martha, `battle.rs`,
as in the played run): checkpoint 252979 -> 240967 (**-12012 f, -200 s**, mostly different blackout luck, so not attributable
to the Repel alone); wild encounters on the surviving branch **0** (before: Geodude catch, Mankey and Geodude flees); 0 bumps.
Caveat found: a Repel only turns away wilds BELOW
the first non-fainted party member's level (`IsWildLevelAllowedByRepel`, `wild_encounter.c:601-620`); when the carry has
fainted and a Lv13 Bellsprout leads, Lv15-17 Rock Tunnel mons pass the Repel. That is a battle / party-order matter.
Not done: delaying the first Repel to the first encounter tile (all of Rock Tunnel is encounter tiles).

crossing (`ObjInfo::poses`) and start only when the whole stretch is clear; left open (outside the four files named).

## 4. Hopeless throws - partly fixed

The brief says "full-HP Metapod"; the r2i events show it was weakened by Bubble to 9/21 HP (34 % per ball, `catch_odds`,
`battle_script_commands.c:9549-9556`) and 5 balls at 34 % fail 13 % of the time: bad luck plus a rule that stops weakening at
half HP (`battle.rs` "Weaken first", not edited). Fix in `dex.rs`: a passing catch is declined when the chance to land one
in the balls in the bag (at most `MAX_THROWS`) at half HP with a Poke Ball is under 0.6 (`PASS_MIN_CHANCE`); logged as a
decision. Suggested for the battle agent: keep weakening while `hp * 3 > max_hp` (Metapod does not attack; a throw costs
~850 f, a Bubble ~410 f).
