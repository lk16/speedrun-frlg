## 1. Why the v2 route caught so little (from `1122-lgdescent-s11-v2/events.jsonl`)

v2 reached the first Hall of Fame with 18 species; the model (`frlg-human dist`, R 2000) has 56.7 at Oak's check.
Causes, in order of species lost:

1. **No passing catches.** The sim catches every new species met on the way whose catch rate is at least
   `pass_min_catch` 45 (`catch_on_pass` defaults true; `sim::run_encounters`, never on `pass_off` segments). The
   controller's `dex::wild_plan` returned the stage's `Flee` outside a hunt, so the Tower, Route 10, Rock Tunnel,
   the Power Plant walk, Route 21's water, the Mansion, Victory Road, ... were all run from.
2. **No balls before Celadon.** Plan 1122 has no `marts`; the first ball purchase was Celadon on fly-celadon-2
   (after Sevii). The Power Plant hunt (4 species) and the Bond Bridge hunt (7 needed) were both skipped,
   "no ball in the bag".
3. **The Safari hunts were never called.** `legs::safari_walk` had no `dex_hook("safari-walk")`: the plan's
   East 90 / West 100 hunts did not run; instead `WildPlan::Catch` threw at everything met on the walk
   (an owned Paras and a second Nidorina caught, 5 Safari Balls).
4. **Evolutions and trades need the mon in the party.** `dex::evolve` / `dex::special` look at the party only;
   every catch after the 6th went to the PC, so 16 of the 31 plan evolutions and both Cinnabar trades logged
   "no pre-evolution in the party" / "not in the party". (Level evolutions are not forced by the controller and
   stay unreachable for non-fighting mons: Pidgeotto, Raticate, Dodrio, Machoke, ... a model optimism.)
5. **Specials without a script**: the Berry Forest Hypno static (fled), the Kakuna/Metapod "gifts" (a model
   abstraction), the fossils (the Helix Fossil sat in the bag from Mt. Moon until the post-game).

Step budgets and the party-full rule were not the binding causes: every hunt that ran with balls finished on its
`stop_after` or on `complete`.

## 2. Controller changes (commits 806bd217, e445a9ce, 5e1d... see git log)

- `dex::wild_plan` -> `pass_plan`: a passing encounter is caught by the sim's own rule (`sim::wants_catch` with
  `want_catch = catch_on_pass && catch_rate >= pass_min_catch && seg not in pass_off`) when a ball is in the bag
  (Safari Balls in the zone); otherwise fled and logged `model-vs-actual`.
- `legs::safari_walk`: HM03 and the Gold Teeth first (so the zone's 600 steps can run out safely), then the plan's
  hunts West then East (logged: the plan's order is East, West); the walk itself catches only new species.
- `shop::mart_topup` at plan 1413's mart stops (model-honesty-2): Lavender Great Balls to 15 (after the heal),
  Cinnabar to 30 + 5 Hyper Potions (new stage `cinnabar-dex` between `mansion` and `blaine`), Celadon 2F to 35
  (replacing `ball_batch` 20 on top), Saffron Hyper Potions +7 (1413's sabrina-badge 3 + silph-walk 4).
- `dexfin::errands(center, place, reserve)`: the dex finish's RAM planner (party + 14 boxes + bag + money) run at
  centers the route already visits, restricted to the PC (stones / <=2 Rare Candies in the bag, party or boxed
  mons) and the town's trades, fossils and stone purchases; up to three passes (Pikachu -> Raichu -> Electrode).
  Called at Fuchsia (Koga's heal), Cinnabar (`cinnabar-dex`), Celadon (shopping, stones keep ¥30 000 in hand).
  `keep_slots` now also keeps the Surf / Strength users and the pre-evolution of a plan stone evolution still
  pending (Eevee for the erika-badge Jolteon). Eevee is excluded from errands.
- The model's heal after Koga (`center-heal@koga-badge`) when the lead leaves the gym below half HP (first dev pass:
  Blastoise fainted to Koga, the Power Plant was walked with Nidoqueen leading and the Mansion blacked out).
- Strength carrier deposit never takes a keep slot (the v2 code deposited "the last slot" without Doduo).
- `battle::weaken_move` counts every hit of a multi-hit move (Double Kick "27-32 of 43" KO'd a Magnemite);
  controller-only (the sim does not call it). From the HoF on.
- Walker: tiles bumped on two walks of a stage stay blocked for the stage (`Session::bumped`, cleared at each stage
  start, so resumes plan identically); a hunt paces another pair when one of its tiles was bumped
  (pg-canon5: Victory Road 2F (33, 17) bumped 57 times by one fill hunt).
- Post-HoF fill (`legs3::fill_round`): stops hunting as soon as `dexfin::plan` reaches 60 from the bag/party/boxes.

Not changed (calibration items 6, 7): idle/turn overhead and the bag navigation of ball throws. The idle is the
human model's reaction draws after battles and at direction changes; the bag already keeps its pocket and row
between throws (the 690-880 f per Great Ball miss is mostly the game's own throw/shake animation).

## Model bugs (running list)

1. **Passing catches were the model's biggest dex source and the controller had none.** Not a model bug, a
   controller one; but the model's per-species cost of a passing catch should be checked against these ~1 000-2 000
   frame catches (`catch_end_new`, `throw_frames`).
2. **Level evolutions of non-fighting mons** (Pidgeotto, Raticate, Dodrio, Machoke, Dugtrio, Sandslash, Persian,
   Venomoth, Weezing, Slowbro, Kakuna, Metapod) are banked by the model (`evolutions` via level, `specials` "gift"
   requires Weedle / Caterpie); in the game a boxed Lv5-25 mon never levels. ~10 of the model's 56.7.
3. **Plan 1122 has no ball stops** (`marts`), yet its hunts and passing catches assume balls; 1413's stops work.
4. **The Safari's East hunt duplicates the West one** for this plan's needs (West has every East species except
   Paras, owned, and Pinsir): East cost 10 882 frames for one Nidoran F that the West also offers.
5. **The Koga heal**: the model heals after the badge, the controller before (low PP after the Safari); with both
   needed at times, the model's single heal is optimistic.
