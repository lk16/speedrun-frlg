## 2. Time variance

Runner, deep run of the 2026-11-12 stream against the LG PB, 13 windows Brock .. Giovanni 3: mean +7 s, **sd 36 s, rms 7.3%** of a
10.9 min window (the PB is a best run, so the per-run sigma is between 7.3% and 7.3%/sqrt(2) = 5.2%). Model (`frlg-human splits`, WINDOW rows,
no resets): 13 front windows rms **6.5%**, the 13 League/post-game windows 6.7% but 1.3-4.4% for Icefall, Warehouse, Lorelei 2 .. Agatha 2
(near-deterministic walks and text). So the front half is already as wide as the runner's windows; nothing is changed there. Two additions, both
off by default:

* `win_cv` 0.033: mean-preserving lognormal noise on every WR split window (the quadrature gap 7.3% vs 6.5%), which lifts the near-deterministic
  windows to 3.5-5% and leaves the wide ones near their own value. Weak evidence (the upper end of the runner's range).
* `slow` events: the heavy tail the runner describes (Safari 3:20 for two mons / "lost 2-3 min", Power Plant duplicates ~2 min, five cave
  encounters ~1:30, a missed bike line ~40 s, spec-strat fallback 1.5-2 min; STRATEGY section 8). Costs are the quotes, the **probabilities are guesses**
  (a Safari 20%, Power Plant 30%, cave extra 100% x 40 s, Route 8 15%, kick fallback 6% at Misty and the Cerulean rival). Mean +1.8 min per run;
  no-reset p10 / p50 / p90 3:24:24 / 3:29:54 / 3:36:46 -> 3:25:48 / 3:31:44 / 3:39:07.

## 3. Per-segment hazards (`fatal`)

`fatal` is an unsalvaged death in a segment (League: back to the first room; mid-game: the pace and the items are gone), applied only in evaluation
runs (a rung is set), never in the pilot, `splits` or `dist`. `tools/fit_hazards.py` runs the plan at the lenient rung (WR+160m, no pace resets to
speak of) without `fatal`, takes the model's survival per window, and spreads the missing survival over the window's fights in log space.
Window totals are data (with partial pooling, k = 6 toward the 22% per-window death share of the windows after Misty, so a window with 0 of 10
does not become safe); the split inside a window is a guess (weights in the tool: the `!deaths` list sammy x2, liam, brock x5 for the first window; Koga
"a huge problem"; the others equal-ish). The old guessed `hazard` rows (0.035 / 0.017 / 0.017 / 0.086) and `regime_bad` are removed: the fitted
hazards are pooled over good and bad days. Values: forest-trainers .181, pewter-gym-trainer .095, brock .393, route3-trainers/mt-moon-miguel .021,
misty/rival-cerulean .041, rival-ss-anne .059, rival-tower .043, koga .127, blaine .200, mansion-trainers/three-island-paxton .072, rival-silph .070,
sabrina/erika .103, giovanni .070, rival-route22 .199, victory-road .071 (the plan file carries the full map and the evidence per row).

Post-door there is **no direct evidence** (one finish, four pace-gate resets, no observed death). The shape (Lance and Champion twice a Lorelei) is a guess;
the total survival `S` is the one calibration residual: it is the value that puts the attempts per WR finish at 15 811 given the rest.

## 4. Calibration: what each piece buys, and what is left unidentified

The target is attempts per WR finish: the runner's 15 811 (a geometric first-passage time, one observation: the 95% interval for the true rate spans
1.4e-6 to 3e-4 per attempt, so the target is a point in a wide interval and nothing sharper than a factor of ~3 should be read into it). The pieces,
each applied on top of the previous one to the 452 policy (WR rung, paired seeds):

| step | EHT(WR) | attempts per finish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 452 on the audited model | 59.8 h | 151 |
| + window hazards (`fatal`, fitted: 60 attempts) | 359 h | 1 556 |
| + `slow` events (+1.8 min a run, guessed probabilities) | 932 h | 4 178 |
| + post-door hazards, S = 0.258 (calibration residual) | 3 658 h | 16 410 |
| + `win_cv` 0.033 and S re-solved to 0.183 (**plan 480**) | **3 377 h** | **15 094** |

Checks that were not used to fit: the model's door share of attempts at the lenient rung is 1.7% (the stream day: 1 door in 45 attempts = 2.2%; the table above: 8.3%
of the 1/3 of attempts that pass the starter gate = 2.8%); P(finish | door) with no pace resets is 12.6% (the runner: 1 in 5, n = 5); P(finish <= WR | door) is 0.39%
(the runner ~0.23%, interval 0.04-1.3%). The model is no longer a factor 100 too fast; it sits on the runner's number by construction at the last step and inside
the runner's intervals on the three checks.

**What is not identified.** The post-door survival S and the time tail trade against each other: with no `slow` events S = 0.088 reproduces the same 15.8k
attempts; `slow_scale` 2.4 (mean +4.3 min, finishers' median 3:34) needs S ~ 1 (both solved before `win_cv` was added, which lowers each S a little). The runner's finishes (3:25:25, 3:26:31, 3:27:20, 3:29:05, 3:31:00)
argue against a median finisher at 3:34 and the one-in-five door-to-finish argues against S below ~0.3, which is why the middle is the primary; it is a
choice. One-factor sensitivity (R = 3.2M, seed 5, each row changes one thing; EHT(WR) in hours): `win_cv` 0 -> 4 992; `slow_scale` 0 / 2 -> 1 398 / 6 541;
post-door S 0.10 / 0.35 / 1.0 -> 6 391 / 1 709 / 607; Brock pass at the interval ends 29% / 53% -> 3 944 / 3 065; mid and late window hazards x0.5 / x1.5 -> 1 656 /
7 870. The absolute EHT is uncertain by a factor of ~2.5 either way; the descent below is repeated at the extremes (section 5) to see whether the plan
ranking survives.

## What this does not do

* `fatal` is flat: a hazard does not respond to the plan's tactics (a safer Brock, an extra heal). A policy that lowers the real death rate cannot be
  found by the descent; it can only trade time and resets against a fixed attrition. The old `hazard` had the same property.
* The windows after Misty rest on 3-14 attempts each, pooled; Wilson intervals of 0.2-0.9 on the pass rates. The late death split between fights is a guess.
* Door arrivals (n = 5) tell nothing about the post-door League; S is a residual.
* `regime_bad` is not applied: the pooled hazards already mix the runner's good and bad days (his Brock pass rate: 75% good, 17% bad, 40% pooled).

finishes, `FRLG_MINGAIN` the CI's upper end must also clear 1% of the parent's score; the defaults are the old rules), screened at **WR+2m** (WR itself has 100-500

runner's data say. Nothing here is a claim about the runner's EHT: his policy is the 480 baseline, not 492, and the real tail of runs that are 3-4 minutes behind at
Koga is not in the data (n = 60 attempts, one deep run).
