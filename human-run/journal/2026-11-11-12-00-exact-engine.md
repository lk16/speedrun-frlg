# 2026-11-11/12: the mechanics-exact engine replaces the estimates

User direction for this session: *"too much estimation — hard numbers: emulate RNG and
battles in code, RNG advancing for walking and NPC movement"*, then test the WR run's
techniques (Squirtle-only, FireRed, SpA gate, Torrent, no fishing, Abra teleport, escape
ropes, Mewtwo).

## What was built (commits 65373b3..3d32a7a)

1. **Game RNG**: `GameRng` wraps the real LCG (`frlg-rng`, emulator-verified), advanced
   once per modelled frame (`src/main.c:412`) by `bump()` — walking, text and battles all
   move the same stream a battle later draws from. Re-keyed per segment for CRN pairing;
   the doc comment in `rng.rs` argues why that is statistically indistinguishable from
   continuity for a random 16-bit timer seed (`src/main.c:264`) plus human jitter.
2. **Battle engine** (`battle.rs`, rewritten): the decomp's exact damage order (badge
   boosts incl. the previously missed Thunder-Badge ×1.1 *speed* boost,
   `battle_main.c:3444`; stat stages `pokemon.c:1442`; burn/screens/`+2`; crit ×2 then
   STAB ×15/10 then type chart then `(100 − Random()%16)/100`), accuracy stages, crits
   {16,8,4,3,2}, sleep 2-5 / freeze 20% thaw / para 1-in-4 / confusion self-hits / Toxic,
   ~60 move effect classes, X items (+1 stage, `item_effects.h`), enemy Full Restores
   below 1/4 (`ShouldUseItem`), and the trainer AI ported from `battle_ai_scripts.s`
   (CheckBadMove vetoes; TryToFaint +4/+6 and −1 for not-most-powerful with the AI's own
   rolled damage estimates; the CheckViability handlers for the effects route trainers
   carry; ties broken by `Random() % n` — which is *why* gen-3 AI mixes status moves with
   attacks). Approximations are listed in the module doc.
3. **Route-sim integration**: 4-move-slot management (level-ups auto-offered, TMs/tutors
   as plan events, keep-list), X-item stock and purchases (Celadon 5F), per-fight tactics
   (X items, heal threshold, sacrificial switches), status persisting out of battle and
   cured by the matching item, catch odds on the game stream with the exact status bonus
   (×2 sleep/freeze, ×1.5 rest; `battle_script_commands.c:9553-9556`), wild IVs/nature
   from the real rolls (`pokemon.c` CreateBoxMon), wild Abra Teleport-flees, Mewtwo with
   rolled IVs and its real L70 moves (Swift/Recover/Safeguard/Psychic) plus re-taught HMs.
4. **Player policy** (the simulated human, full knowledge): best-expected-damage move;
   attack instead of anything else when a min-roll kill exists; heal only when healing
   actually leaves the enemy's max-roll KO range (the first version Full-Restore-locked
   against the Champion Venusaur's OHKO SolarBeam — worth remembering: every policy rule
   needs a "does this action change the outcome" guard).
