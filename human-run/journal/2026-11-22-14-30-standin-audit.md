## Where the stand-ins are

Death before the League comes from the **risk lane** (`risk_fight`, every fight up to `victory-road`): the same
fight again at the formula's damage times a power factor, a loss ends the attempt (`risk_abandon`). The factor is
the fight's own `fight_tactics.X.risk_power` if set, else its `risk_windows` power, else `risk_power` 1.0. Up to
`no_salvage_until` (rival-ss-anne) a loss of the timing lane (`carry_power` 2.0) is also an abandon. In 1202:

- per-fight, hand-set: `misty` 2.0, `rival-cerulean` 3.5 (`rival-ss-anne` already 1.0 since 1200);
- `risk_windows`, **fitted** to the runner's windowed survival (`tools/fit_lane.py`, `video-analysis/data/accepted-runs.json`,
  63 attempts): until rock-tunnel-trainers 1.111 (this window also covers every fight before Misty), snorlax 2.565,
  koga-badge 1.35, blaine/sevii 0.773, giovanni-badge 1.572, indigo-heal 2.314;
- timing lane: `carry_power` 2.0 (fitted to the WR video's action counts), Koga / Sabrina / Giovanni `power` .667 with
  `free_torrent` (mechanism: the WR enters them inside Torrent, `v2-mid-fights.md`);
- League: `league_power` 1.0 (already honest), `mewtwo_power` 1.3 (fitted to the video's R2 action counts,

## Findings

1. **Rival Cerulean: the stand-in hid a ~25-30% death.** Engine and emulator agree at the s11 party (60-69% vs
   68%); the sim lane at the plan party is 74%. It is Bulbasaur L18's Sleep Powder then Vine Whip (all seven
   emulator losses), so the Awakenings 1201 bought matter here too (engine +9 points). The tactic (1 potion,
   heal only at KO range) was descended while the fight was free; 3 Super Potions and heal below 35% recover
   11 h. Refuted responses: a helper sacrifice (+7.5, the runner's Spearow tank does not pay in the model),
   5 Awakenings, heal below .5, `fight_until_level` 19/21 (+26.8), candy_early_n 2 (n.s.).
2. **Misty's 2.0 was mostly the Persim Berry.** The plan paid the berry's frames (`persim-and-potions`) but the
   engine never used it. `persim_held` (new MODEL CHANGE key, `battle_util.c:2766,2913`, test
   `a_held_persim_cures_the_first_confusion_only`) makes it cure the first confusion until `black-glasses-swap`:
   with it Misty at power 1.0 costs +2.6 h n.s. The emulator (33/34) cannot separate 94% from 99.6%.
3. **The plan's carry is 4-5 levels behind the played s11 carry mid-game**: L32 vs L36 at Giovanni 1, L33 vs L38 at the
   tower rival, L37 vs L42 at Koga (the WR is L41 at Koga). s11 played this very route. At the s11 levels the engine
   wins the hideout Giovanni and the tower rival 99-100% at power 1.0, as the emulator does (40/40). So the 2.565
   window is not "runner skill", it is mostly a **missing exp economy** in the sim (the 2026-11-21 audit saw the same
   3-level gap at Koga against the WR). Kept: the runner's 12/12 survival demands it, and removing it before the
   levels are fixed would be dishonest the other way.
4. **Koga: the engine is too deadly.** At the s11 party's exact stats the engine wins 35% (no X: 9%), the emulator with
   the same policy 15/20. Losses in both are to Muk (Minimize / Toxic / Acid Armor: 26 of 64 emulator attacks on Muk did
   no damage). The 1.35 window brings the engine to 70%, i.e. it stands in for an engine/AI discrepancy at Muk, not for
   skill. Kept; open bug (NEXT).
5. **The 0.773 window (Blaine / Sevii) is below the engine**: the fitted lane is deadlier than the honest engine
   (Paxton 16% vs 7.7%), worth -21 h if set to 1.0, and the honest window (.83) is inside the runner's interval (n=9,
   several of the runner's "deaths" there are pace resets: 87.7, 89.7 "bad Safari", 92.7 min). It is a fit, so kept,
   but it is the one stand-in that errs pessimistic.
6. `fit_lane`'s first window covers every fight from the start (`risk_win` has no lower bound), so the
   misty-rocktunnel fit also sets Brock and Mt. Moon. Harmless now (Brock 31.6% at 1.0 and 1.111), but the forest-brock
   window is .59 against the runner's .41 [.30, .54]: the model is optimistic before Brock, independent of powers.
