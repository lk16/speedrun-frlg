Reading: in the League a Full Restore is worth its 2.5 Hyper Potions. Every status move (Jynx's Lovely Kiss,
Agatha's Hypnosis / Confuse Ray) is answered in one item turn that also refills HP; a Hyper Potion bag trades
that for HP the carry rarely lacks. A Full Heal on a half-HP fighter costs a second item turn later (fh1 vs fh1b).
So the best count is **"as many Full Restores as the purse buys, up to 14"**, plus the 4 Saffron Hyper Potions
(2 leans better on two seeds, n.s.; not adopted).
