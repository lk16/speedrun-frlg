# 2026-11-14 (night): rebuilding the fill / catch economy of the WR-order model

Task: in plan 376 the Giovanni 3 -> Start E4R1 window took 30:46 against the WR's 12:12, "Start E4R1" was +13.9%, the
model held 43 species at Giovanni 3 against ~51.5, and fills cost ~85-91 s per species. Branch work only; plans
390..430 (LeafGreen, `CURRENT`) and 440..447 (FireRed, `CURRENT-FR`). Everything the model decides about the dex is now a
plan knob and was searched with paired evaluation.

## Decomp facts this rests on (all read this session)

* **Gate.** Oak's check is `VAR_0x8009 >= 60` where 0x8009 is the Kanto *caught* count
  (`data/scripts/pokedex_rating.inc:35-45`, `src/prof_pc.c:23-26`, `src/pokedex.c:75-93`, `KANTO_DEX_COUNT = 151`
  `include/constants/pokedex.h:424`) and `FLAG_WORLD_MAP_ONE_ISLAND` (`PalletTown_ProfessorOaksLab/scripts.inc:904-908`).
  Only species 1..151 count, flags not bodies: a traded-away or evolved mon stays counted.
* **What sets the caught flag:** a catch (`battle_script_commands.c:9657`), `givemon` gifts (`script_pokemon_util.c:67`),
  any evolution (`evolution_scene.c:783,1217`), and in-game trades (`trade_scene.c:1042`). So evolutions, trades and gifts are
  dex currency; none needs a ball.
* **One evolution per battle.** `TryEvolvePokemon` clears the mon's level-up bit before the scene (`battle_main.c:3880-3907`), so
  Weedle -> Kakuna -> Beedrill needs two level-ups (a candy or a second fight), not one.
* **In-game trade table** (`src/data/ingame_trades.h`, scripts in `data/maps/<map>/scripts.inc`; every received species is in the
  Kanto dex): Spearow -> Farfetch'd (`VermilionCity_House2`), Venonat -> Tangela and Raichu -> Electrode
  (`CinnabarIsland_PokemonLab_Lounge`), Abra -> Mr. Mime (`Route2_House`), Ponyta -> Seel
  (`CinnabarIsland_PokemonLab_ExperimentRoom`), Poliwhirl -> Jynx (`CeruleanCity_House3`), Slowbro (LG) / Golduck (FR) -> Lickitung
  (`Route18_EastEntrance_2F`), **version-specific**: Nidoran F -> Nidoran M (LG) / M -> F (FR) at `UndergroundPath_NorthEntrance`
  (Route 5), Nidorina -> Nidorino (LG) / Nidorino -> Nidorina (FR) at `Route11_EastEntrance_2F`. A trade gives the given mon away,
  so the model now tracks bodies: a Nidorina traded for a Nidorino cannot also become a Nidoqueen.
* **Free evolution stones on the route** (map.json item balls / hidden items): Moon at Mt. Moon 1F and B2F, Rocket Hideout B2F,
  Mansion 1F, and Two Island (`TwoIsland_JoyfulGameCorner/scripts.inc:164-170`, forced); Leaf in the Safari (East ball, Center hidden);
  Thunder in the Power Plant. Fire and Water are only bought (Celadon 4F `CeladonCity_DepartmentStore_4F/scripts.inc:31-34`, 2100).
* **Free Rare Candies** (item balls): Warden's House, Mt. Moon 1F, Hideout B3F, Tower 6F, Silph 10F, Victory Road 1F; hidden: Cerulean,
  Routes 6, 9, 12, 17, Mansion 3F, Cape Brink. "Warden candy" of the transcripts is `FuchsiaCity_WardensHouse/map.json:58`.
* **Gift levels** the old plan had at 30: Eevee/Lapras/Hitmonlee 25, Magikarp/fossils 5, Abra 9 FR / 7 LG, Clefairy 8 FR / 12 LG
  (`CeladonCity_GameCorner_PrizeRoom/scripts.inc:122-141`), prices Abra 180/120 coins, Clefairy 500/750.
* Old Amber is a free item from the museum scientist (`PewterCity_Museum_1F/scripts.inc:144-151`), revived at the Cinnabar lab like
  the Sevii fossil: the Pewter detour is Aerodactyl + Diglett/Dugtrio + the Mr. Mime trade.

## What was built

`crates/frlg-human` (all knobs default-off, so the old plans score as before; the WR order's Bond Bridge walk used a site name
that matches no table and had no encounters, fixed for `route_style = "wr"` only):

* `evolutions`: `{after, species, via: stone|candy|level, item, buy}`. Needs a *body* of the pre-evolution (species table), a stone from the
  stock (`elixirs` kinds `moon/leaf/thunder/fire/water`) or a purchase (`buy`, 2100 and the shop frames), candies from the stock (one per level
  short of the evolution level, at least one), or, for `level`, a pre-evolution caught within `level_gap` levels (the mon takes part in a fight).
  Frames: stone 1100, candy 700 + 300 each, level 2000 (guesses).
* Catch levels, catch order, bodies and the source of each entry (`frlg-human bag` prints them); `pc_frames` charges a PC visit once per anchor
  when a needed mon was obtained after the party of six filled (1500 frames).
* `safari_stop` (leave the zone for good after N new species), `Hunt.stop_after`, `poke_from`/`ultra_below` (ball by catch rate under the money
  model), `skip_powerplant`, `pass_off` (no passing catches on listed segments), `level_gap`.
* `frlg-human bag <plan> --after <segment>`: species share, mean catch level, candies, money, entries by source. `dist` also lists what is in the bag
  when the fill starts.
* `human-run/tools/dexknobs.py` + `descend.py --set dex [--kinds ...]`: catalogue of evolutions (pruned by the bag), trades, pickups, Safari target and
  area order, Power Plant skip, Pewter bundle, ball rule, route hunts, pass_off, candy_early_n, version, reset thresholds. `descend.py` also had a bug:
  its candidates replace whole lists from the plan at the start of the pass, so an accept overwrote the previous accept (plan 392 lost 391's Dodrio).
  It now rebuilds the candidates after every promotion.
* Test `evolutions_need_a_body_and_a_stock`.

## What remains / honest limits

* Frames of every new event are guesses (stone 1100, candy 700 + 300, level 2000, trade 2200-3000, PC visit 1500, pickups 300-700); the Pewter bundle
  (3600) and the Nidoran/Nidorino trade detours are guesses; all decomp citations are for *what exists and where*, not for how long it takes.
* `level` evolutions let a mon within 8 levels of its evolution level evolve for 2000 frames and no exp bookkeeping; one stage per battle (cited above) is
  not enforced for two `level` events at the same anchor.
* Ball economy: still on-demand buying (`marts` empty) apart from the ball rule; the LG single Cinnabar shop and the 1-3 early Poke Balls are not a plan
  shape, and money was never binding (27k at Koga). Stone purchases are charged.
* Fill hunts (`fill` list) are barely used now (the plan reaches 60 with them idle in most attempts); they were not retuned.
* The model's post-E4R1 half is still faster than the WR (End +2.5% against +8.7% at the door) while the first half is +6-12% slower: a calibration
  residual of the fight engine / `carry_power`, not of this work.
* Not done: Pewter skip with a different Strength carrier, Warden-candy skip as a lever on its own (the candy pickups are individually toggled and the search
  kept all of them), a Mart schedule, the LG "deferred catches" order (catches placed after Erika) as a whole-plan shape: hunts could be moved there by
  `dexknobs` but the search never preferred it.
