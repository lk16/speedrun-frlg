## 1. The end of the dex fill (`play/dexfin.rs`, stages `dex-finish-1..8`, `dex-finish-party`)

At `dex-fill-24` the greedy hunt was down to sites with one uncaught species at 6-55 % of the encounters.
The last species come cheaper from what the bag, party and boxes already hold. `dexfin::plan` reads
RAM (owned flags, party, the 14 boxes with box levels from experience, `experience_tables.h`; bag;
money) and lists every action that adds a species:

- stone evolutions with stones in the bag (`EVO_ITEM` of the species data), on party or boxed mons,
  and on mons a trade would bring (the traded Nidorino -> Nidoking);
- Rare Candy evolutions (`EVO_LEVEL` within the candies in the bag; only a species that is not also
  in the party, since the candy goes on a party slot by species);
- in-game trades of this version (`src/data/ingame_trades.h`; LG: Farfetch'd for Spearow, Nidorino
  for Nidorina, Lickitung for Slowbro, ...) when the requested mon is owned;
- the Magikarp salesman (`Route4_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc:23-53`, ¥500);
- fossils in the bag (`CinnabarIsland_PokemonLab_ExperimentRoom/scripts.inc`: give, step into the
  Entrance (`..._REVIVE_STATE` 1 -> 2 on its `OnTransition`), back, `givemon` -- to the PC when the
  party is full, the caught flag is set either way, `src/script_pokemon_util.c:48-73`);
- stones bought at the Celadon 4F (`BuyStone`).

It picks the cheapest set of **places** (every subset; place and action costs are ranking guesses,
not model constants) whose actions reach 60, then visits them in a fixed loop. Each stage re-plans
from RAM and does one place, so a death costs one place; the last stage puts the fill's party back
(Lapras for Strength/Waterfall, the Cut and Fly users).

Plan chosen at 51 caught (¥4 934, Mt. Moon B2F): Ninetales, Victreebel, Wigglytuff (stones), Primeape
(Rare Candy on a boxed Mankey) and the Magikarp at the Route 4 center (two PC sessions there),
Farfetch'd (Vermilion), Nidorino + Nidoking (Route 11 gate, Moon Stone), Omanyte (Cinnabar).
Canonical `pg-canon5` (resumed from `pg-canon4` `dex-fill-24`): **60 caught at frame 1 090 784,
0 rollbacks**, 30 188 frames (505 s) from `dex-fill-24`; party restored at the Cinnabar center;
**National Dex at 18 357 s** (frame 1 097 027; Oak's scene, `PalletTown/scripts.inc:41-57`, lab
