Task: the fitted per-segment `fatal` hazards (Brock pass ~40%, Brock->Misty 64%, Misty->Rock Tunnel 71%) were flat, so no tactic could lower them. This entry

## 1. Why the engine passed Brock 98% and the runner 40%

Probes (`frlg-human battle TRAINER_LEADER_BROCK 11`, Brock = Geodude L12 + Onix L14, Tackle/Defense Curl, Tackle/Bind/Rock Tomb: `decompiled/src/data/trainer_parties.h`
`sParty_LeaderBrock`) and the trace of the new lane (`FRLG_RISK_TRACE=1`) say it was three modelling choices, not a missing mechanic:

1. **Infinite free potions.** `Tactics::heal_stock` is `u32::MAX` outside the League. The harness at 10/32 HP (the WR's Brock state) wins 32% with `--heals 0` and ~99% with unlimited
   potions. The runner's rule is "never heal in Brock" (STRATEGY s4: skip the PC potion, "that is why you never heal").
2. **`carry_power` 2.0 in every early fight.** It is a fitted stand-in for the human's play (journal 2026-11-16-10-00); at honest damage the Cerulean/S.S. Anne rivals (Bulbasaur team,
   resists Water) and Misty are the deadly fights (58-68% win), at 2.0 they are ~95%. Brock itself is insensitive to power (Water is 4x on both mons); it is a pure HP/range fight.
3. **The model human sits at a safe HP.** Brock lane win rate by HP at entry with no potion: 0.3-0.4: 13-26%, 0.5: 29%, 0.6: 46%, 0.7: 80%, >= 0.8: ~100%. The runner enters at about 1/3 HP for
   Torrent (STRATEGY s4, Rock Tomb 8-10 vs 5-7 after Withdraw), which is exactly the 13-46% band; the "range" he talks about is this table.

Other loss modes named in the transcripts (Quick Attack crits, Bide/Bind misses, Rock Tomb speed drops, Pikachu crits, poison, "3 HP 10 Def") are in the engine (crits, accuracy, status,
speed stages, the starter's real IVs/nature); misclicks are not, and no noise term was needed (below).

## 2. Model change: the risk lane (`sim.rs risk_fight`, params `risk_*`)

Every fight up to `risk_until` (`rival-ss-anne`) is played a second time, honestly: carry power `risk_power` (1.0), foes `risk_foe` (1.0), HP from its own lane (the timing lane's HP plus what
`carry_power` hid; the timing lane's free potions are not inherited), a per-fight potion cap (`risk_potions` 3, `FightTactic.potions`), potions **paid** from the purse (300/700/1200 by heal tier) and one bag
turn each (`risk_pay`). A loss in the lane is an unsalvaged death (`no_salvage_until rival-ss-anne` abandons). Timing lane, splits and all calibrations are untouched. The flat `fatal` of
forest-trainers .. rival-ss-anne (nine entries) is removed. The hazard now depends on starter stats (real IVs/nature), level, HP, potions, tactic (`entry`, `heal_below`, `potions`, `sacs`),
and `early_helpers` lets the caught mons be the early sacrifices. Tests: `risk_lane_deaths_respond_to_the_potion_budget_and_the_foes`.

Structural stipulations (not fits): Brock `potions 0` (the runner's rule); Misty, rival-cerulean, rival-ss-anne at `risk_power 2.0` (the timing-lane stand-in for his skill; honest power kills them 40-50%,
the runner's own per-fight hazards there were 4-6%). Result with **no fitted noise** (`risk_foe` 1.0; `tools/fit_risk.py`, R = 300k):

| window | runner | lane |
| --- | --- | --- |
| start -> Brock split | 24/60 = 0.40 (CI .29-.53) | 0.400 |
| -> Misty split (incl. Cerulean rival) | 14/22 = 0.64 (.43-.80) | 0.588 |

Log-likelihood over `risk_foe` .9/1.0/1.2: -63.5 / -63.4 / -64.3: flat, 1.0 is the maximum. The documented "noise" is therefore the potion cap (3, a guess: 2 and 5 also fit,
LL -63.7 / -63.3) and the `risk_power 2.0` stand-in. A free 6-potion stock (instead of the cap) pushes the Brock window to 0.18 (wild fights drain it): not used.

  explain why he does it: it has **no Torrent credit** beyond the in-fight x1.5 (Route 3 / Mt. Moon weakening, STRATEGY s4 "Torrent for Route 3", is a constant `weaken_hp`). If Torrent at Route 3 is worth
  more than the model says, 932-934 are optimistic. Next: a Torrent-dependent weaken/catch rate and Route 3 time, then re-run `epot` at Brock.
* Window 2 is a little harsh (0.59 vs 0.64), window 3 a little lenient; all inside the runner's CIs (n = 60/22/14). Post-Misty hazards are still flat `fatal`.
* The lane's potion cap and `risk_power 2.0` are stand-ins; `risk_pay` charges the lane's potions but the timing lane's own heals stay free.
