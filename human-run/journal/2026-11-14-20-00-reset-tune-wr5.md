## 1. Starter/reset model vs `STRATEGY.md` and `video-analysis/data/starters` (no model change)

* Wall cost of a miss: the model gives 268 s (183 s to the stats screen incl. the 15 s tier idle, + 100 s reset + 45 s boot);
  the scans (11 sessions) give start-to-start of a rejected attempt median 265-335 s, mean 318 s (35 attempts, one session),
  reject timer median 2:11-2:25. So the model sits at the median, not the mean. `s_reset` +50 s (mean) costs +0.66 h, -50 s
  gives -0.66 h at WR+5m (paired, R = 160 000, CI +-0.03): a calibration offset, not a design lever. Kept.
* Early abort: `starter-manip.md` 4.3 assumes a miss is seen at the press (timer program reports ms error). The scan data
  say otherwise: of 248 rejected readings only 3 are REJECTED_TITLE and 6 REJECTED_PRERUN; the rest reset at the stats
  screen/lab (timer 1:50-2:30). Stage-wise abort is rare in practice, so the stats-screen reject is the right model.
* Thresholds: accepted starters (n = 64 with IVs read) have SpA >= 30 in 63/64, Spe >= 20 in all (p10 24, mean 27), HP
  min 4 (10/64 below 12). The model's min_spe 20 / min_hp 12 are consistent; the runner's HP rule is softer than a hard
  floor. Re-swept at WR+5m (screen seed 1, R = 160 000, vs 389): min_spe 24 +0.86 ns, 26 +2.9; min_hp 8 +0.22 ns, 16 +1.0;
  min_spa 31 +3.4; min_def 10 +2.7; quiet_max_hits 3 +0.38, 5 +0.01 ns. All refuted or ns: the thresholds are flat/optimal.
* Per-try structure: one attempt per hard reset (reset -> boot -> title press -> New Game); the 60-roll quiet window is
  capped (engine fix of the previous session) and the title wait counts in the wall. No change.
* Hit rate is the real lever: sigma 0.3 (p = 0.9, the doc's good regime) -3.4 h, sigma 0.8 (p ~ 0.5) +16 h (WR+5m).

## 2. Per-segment salvage rule (engine: `salvage_at`, `salvage_default`, `league_redo` in `Params`)

On a whiteout in a fight the human resets when `clock + whiteout cost + floor + a * (mean - floor) > T` (the split-reset formula
with its own `a` per segment), else continues; `league_redo > 0` replays the League rooms (cost = `league_redo` x League
clock so far) instead of resetting. The pilot (no target) keeps the old behaviour so `mean_after` is unchanged (first version
inflated it and destroyed all finishes). Default params leave every existing plan bit-identical (checked, diff 0.000).

All refuted at WR+10m (seed 1, R = 40 000, vs 386; `scores.jsonl`):
* `salvage_default` 0.8 / 0.94 / 1.0 (reset on any whiteout past ss-anne when over budget): +0.70 / +1.04 / +1.42 h.
* `league_redo` 1.0 / 1.5: +0.08 / +0.26 h; with the immediate budget check on the E4 members: -0.03 ns (17 more finishes of
  1794, no EHT gain: a redo costs the whole League clock, more than a fresh reset at this rung).
* Per-segment `salvage_at` (0.9 and 1.2) overriding `no_salvage_until` for forest-trainers, pewter-gym-trainer, brock,
  route3-trainers, mt-moon-extra, mt-moon-miguel, cerulean-gym-trainer, misty, rival-cerulean, nugget-bridge-trainers,
  route25-trainers, rival-ss-anne: all +0.0 to +0.26 h or ns (best: brock 1.2 -0.045 CI [-0.10, +0.015]).
* `no_salvage_until` re-checked at WR+5m: off +3.7 h, koga +1.5, misty +0.8, nugget-bridge-trainers +0.35 ns. rival-ss-anne stays.

## Caveats

* The WR+5m tail is thin (~1% of attempts finish); `reset_aggr` 0.3 globally means "trust the floor" and rests on the model's
  run-time spread being right. The no-reset distribution (min 3:23:23, p50 3:36:26) is unchanged, so this is policy, not
  a faster run. Per `PROMPT.md`'s sanity alarm: no finish under the WR was produced.
* `mistake_rate` is a calibration (0.3); 0.2 gives -7.3 h and 0.4 +9.9 h at WR+5m, so the rung is very sensitive to it and
  to the press hit rate; neither was touched.
