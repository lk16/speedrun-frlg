## API for the route stages

```rust
use super::dex;
use super::shop::{self, PcOp};

// Everything the plan anchors after a model segment, in the model's order (route.rs inserts
// evolutions, then specials, then hunts right after the anchor; each kind in plan order):
dex::dex_hook(s, "safari-walk")?;          // the two Safari hunts (East 90 steps, West 100)
dex::dex_hook(s, "koga-badge")?;           // evolutions anchored there, ...

// Single pieces:
let rep = dex::hunt(s, &s.plan.params.hunts[4].clone())?;   // walks to the site itself
dex::special(s, &sp)?;                     // gift / in-game trade / Magikarp purchase
dex::evolve(s, &evo)?;                     // stone or Rare Candy from the bag on the party mon
let n = dex::caught_count(s);              // Oak's check is caught >= 60 (see below)

shop::buy(s, "MAP_FUCHSIA_CITY_MART", None, &[("ITEM_GREAT_BALL", 20)])?;
shop::buy(s, "MAP_CELADON_CITY_DEPARTMENT_STORE_4F", None, &[("ITEM_THUNDER_STONE", 1)])?;
shop::pc(s, "MAP_FUCHSIA_CITY_POKEMON_CENTER_1F", &[PcOp::Deposit(2), PcOp::Withdraw(species_id)])?;
shop::use_item(s, "ITEM_REPEL")?;          // field items without a target
shop::use_item_on(s, "ITEM_MOON_STONE", slot)?;   // stones, Rare Candy (evolution scene: A only)
shop::talk_at(s, map, x, y)?;              // talk to a person, across a counter if there is one
```

- **Wild encounters during a hunt** (also on the walk to the site) go through `dex::wild_plan`:
  catch exactly when `sim::wants_catch` says so for that species (Kanto, not owned per the
  Pokédex flags in RAM, not in `sim::hunt_skip`), else run. Outside a hunt the stage's own
  `s.wild_plan` applies, as before. Every Safari battle (catch or run) is played by
  `dex::safari_battle`: `fight()` cannot see the Safari menu (below).
- **Leaving a site**: the first of `sim::site_done` (every slot owned or skipped),
  `sim::hunt_stop_new` (`stop_after`, and `safari_stop` across the Safari visit, kept in
  `s.dex.safari_new` / `safari_done`), the step (cast) budget, Safari steps or balls at 0, or the
  player off the site map (whiteout, "time's up").
- **Pacing**: the nearest two neighbouring land-encounter tiles (`Tile::land`, away from people),
  walked back and forth with `walk::go`; water hunts use surfable tiles with `Opts::surf`.
- **Fishing**: the best rod in the bag (logged `model-vs-actual` when it differs from the plan's
  `rod`), registered to SELECT once at the spot (the next `mount_bike` re-registers the bike), a
  tile facing water, SELECT per cast.
- **Specials**: a table species -> (map, object script) for the plan's gifts, trades and the
  Magikarp salesman (`dex::SPECIALS`, scripts cited); yes/no boxes are answered YES until the mon
  is owned, NO after (the nickname prompt); a trade's party menu gets the `requires` mon.
- **Evolutions**: the pre-evolution from `sim::evo_source` among the party; `stone` uses the
  plan's stone if it is in the bag (a `buy: true` stone must be bought first with `shop::buy` at
  the Celadon 4F, `evolve` does not walk to shops); `candy` uses Rare Candies one at a time;
  `level` is only checked.

Shared decision code moved out of `sim.rs` private functions (sim output unchanged, checked:
`ev 1122-lgdescent --rung WR+40m --R 4000 --seed 7` = 40/4000, 31.222 h before and after):
`wants_catch`, `hunt_area`, `hunt_skip`, `is_safari_hunt`, `hunt_stop_new`, `site_done`,
`SafariMon` / `SafariAct` / `next_act` / `flee_factor`, `safari_ball_odds`, `evo_item`,
`evo_source`.

## Test harness

    frlg-human dex-test --state X.state [--seed 11] [--out DIR] --do ACTION [--do ACTION ...]

Actions: `hunt:<i>`, `hunt:<MAP>:<land|water|fish>:<steps>[:<stop_after>]`, `special:<i>`,
`evolve:<i>`, `evo:<SPECIES>:<stone|candy>[:<STONE>]`, `hook:<segment>`,
`buy:<MAP>[@x,y]:<ITEM>=<n>,...`, `deposit:<CENTER>:<slot>`, `withdraw:<CENTER>:<SPECIES>`,
`use:<ITEM>[:<slot>]`, `talk:<MAP>:<script>`, `go:<MAP>[:x:y]`, `fly:<MAPSEC>`, `heal:<CENTER>`,
`bike`, `save:<name>`. It builds a `Session` at the savestate (a throwaway run dir with one
checkpoint at frame 0) so everything runs exactly as in `play`. Outputs `events.jsonl`,
`end.png`, `end.state`, one JSON line per action.

## Model bugs and model-vs-actual found

1. **FRLG fishing has no reel input.** `Fishing7` jumps straight to `FISHING_ON_HOOK`
   (`tStep += 3`, `decompiled/src/field_player_avatar.c:1798-1802`); the A-press window
   (`Fishing8`) and the extra rounds (`Fishing9`) are unreachable. A cast is 60 frames of cast,
   4-10 dots x 20 frames (`Fishing4`/`Fishing5`), a 50% bite (`Fishing6`: `Random() & 1`), and a
   result text that waits for A. The sim's 180 frames per cast (guess) is in range for a miss;
   no "missed the bite" loss exists, so there is nothing for the human's reaction delay to lose.
2. **The plan's `rod` is `none`** while it lists no fishing hunt; the Good Rod in Fuchsia costs
   ~1300 frames if a fishing hunt is ever added (the sim's `Dex::area` uses all ten fishing slots
   for `none`, not the Good Rod's three).
3. **Safari RUN costs ~420 frames** from the decision to the field (11 runs measured, 419-435),
   plus the encounter intro (~260 frames to the first menu). The sim charges 200 (guess,
   `encounter`, Safari branch) after `enc_intro`. With 14 unwanted encounters in 90 East steps
   that is ~3100 frames the model does not have.
4. **Safari hunts take far longer than modelled.** East: 20416 frames for 90 steps + 20
   encounters (194 frames per step at the site, battles included; walking 2956 frames to the
   site); West: 11851 for 25 steps. The 2026-11-22 controller run measured the whole Safari
   (`safari-walk`) at 293 s against the model's 94 + 151 + 85 s; this hunt-only measurement is
   ~540 s for the two hunts alone, against the model's ~236 s. Encounter rate on the East grass:
   20 in 90 steps.
5. **A new species' catch ends in ~1050-1170 frames** after the last ball (shakes, "Gotcha",
   the Pokédex registration page, nickname NO), against the model's `catch_end`. Worth a
   calibration pass with these events (`catch` / `battle` frames are in `events.jsonl`).
6. **Safari factors are confirmed**: the catch / escape factors in `gBattleStruct` at the first
   menu equal the sim's `SafariMon::new` for every mon met (Nidoran M 18/3, Doduo 14/3,
   Parasect 5/5, Exeggcute 7/5, Kangaskhan 3/9, Nidorina 9/5); no `model-vs-actual` fired.
   `tests/dex_rules.rs` pins three of them and the ball chances logged (0.50, 0.41, 0.12).
7. **Catch chances at full HP in the plan's hunts are low**: Kangaskhan 0.08 per Safari Ball
   (fled after 1), Parasect 0.12 (3 balls, not caught). The plan has `safari_rocks` 0; a rock
   doubles the catch factor and the flee factor -- an idea for the optimiser, not a bug.
8. **Weakening finds no safe move** for a L43 Blastoise against L12-18 wild mons
   (`battle::weaken_move`): Goldeen and Diglett were thrown at at full HP (Great Ball 0.50). The
   sim's `weaken` assumes 1-2 turns to 30% HP; with an over-levelled carry the human throws at
   full HP instead (the sim is optimistic about catch chances here, pessimistic about time).
9. **`fight()` cannot see the Safari action menu.** `Observer::battle_controller_is` resolves the
   controller by `SymbolTable::covering`, which returns nothing for the Safari
   `HandleInputChooseAction` (0x080dd57c, 0x184 bytes: four functions share the name), so
   `battle.rs` never sees an action menu in the Safari and mashes (the 2026-11-22 controller
   journal's "threw at whatever appeared"). `dex::safari_menu` compares the address with the
   Safari symbol by size; every Safari battle now goes to `dex::safari_battle`.
10. **Mashed presses get lost on list menus** (the buy list, the bag list): one A on the frame
    after the cursor lands is sometimes ignored. `shop::buy` and `items::bag_use` press A until
    the list lets go.
11. Walking: on the way East -> West in the Safari the planner bumped along row 8 of the East
    area (x 23-33, ~90 frames each, ~950 frames): a tile the layout calls walkable is not
    (`walk.rs`, not touched here). The Route 2 tree at (15, 69) was cut twice.

National Dex unlock condition (S7 check, cited): Oak's scene after the first Hall of Fame,
`decompiled/data/maps/PalletTown/scripts.inc:41-57` (`PalletTown_EventScript_OakRatingScene`):
requires `FLAG_WORLD_MAP_ONE_ISLAND` (been to One Island), then `GetPokedexCount` and
`goto_if_lt VAR_0x8009, 60` -- the caught count (`VAR_0x8006`) must be at least 60.
`dex::caught_count` reads the same owned flags.

## Not handled yet

- Specials without a script in `dex::SPECIALS`: the Hypno static (Berry Forest), the Abra
  prize (needs the Coin Case and coins), fossils (Omanyte, Aerodactyl: two visits to the
  Cinnabar lab), the Kakuna/Metapod "gifts" with `requires` (a model abstraction, not an NPC),
  `sell`. These log `model-vs-actual` and return `false`.
- Untested on real states: in-game trades, gifts with the nickname prompt, the Magikarp purchase,
  water (surf) hunts, Rare Candy evolutions, Repel use, a box switch on deposit (the code walks
  the box chooser to the first box with room), withdrawing from a box other than the current one.
- `evolve` uses party mons only; a pre-evolution in a box needs `shop::pc` withdraw first (the
  sim's `pc_visit` charges `pc_frames` 1500 for it; measured deposit 2051 incl. the walk,
  withdraw 556 inside the center).
- Repel hunts (`repel_level`) are not played (no hunt of the plan uses them); `use_item` covers
  the Repel itself.
- The level evolutions (`via: level`) are not forced: the controller only checks and logs.
- `hunt` walks to off-route sites with `walk::go_map`; flying there is the route's call (`items::fly`).
- The reaction delay of the first decision in a non-Safari wild battle is the battle bridge's
  (it reacts after unpredictable turns only); `wild_plan` logs the decision at the battle start.
