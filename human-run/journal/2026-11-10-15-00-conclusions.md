No plan reaches WR, WR+1m ... WR+20m (fewer than 50 finishes in 200k attempts). The no-reset finishing time of
the bulk plan is p10 4:14, median 4:23, p90 4:35 (min 4:01 in 4000 attempts); the WR is 3:25:25. The model
therefore says the WR needs something this model does not contain (RNG manipulation, a stronger team, better
routing), not a better version of this plan.

Why two plans: the PROMPT's comparison (tighter rung wins) rewards a plan for a handful of lucky finishes at
a tighter rung, and the search then trades the bulk for the tail (the bulk plan's EHT at WR+80m rose from 4.9
to 5.9 h while EHT(WR+40m) fell from hundreds of hours to ~85). Both are reported; the bulk plan is the
one to follow if the goal is "finish under WR+80m reliably", the other if the goal is the WR tail. The
difference is the starter gate (SpA >= 12, total 71 vs SpA >= 10, total 70), a lower catch-rate cutoff for
passing throws (35 vs 45), more Repels, a slightly different fill list.

## What the session established (details in the running notes)

1. **Starter gates on visible level-5 stats** are the biggest single plan lever (EHT 54 h -> ~30 h at the time).
2. **Model corrections, in the order found** (each a MODEL CHANGE line in `scores.jsonl`): rival ids and a data
   bug (039), type matchups (040), sight-cone audit of forced trainers (041, 052), Safari rules (053), money
   and ball tiers (065), encounter costs measured on libmgba (084), fishing rods (087), battle costs measured on
   libmgba (107), grass steps from the map (166), fill-site walking from the map (221). Net: the model got
   *slower* on trainers and encounters, *faster* on routes without grass.
3. **Which starter**: Squirtle 4.50 h, Bulbasaur 4.62 h, Charmander 4.72 h at WR+80m, each re-tuned
   (`CHAIN-BULB`, `CHAIN-CHAR` converged at once). Squirtle ahead by 2-5%: close, and the type model
   (`type_floor`, a guess) drives it.
4. **Abilities** are not a plan choice; Illuminate/Stench change encounter rates but a hunt is dominated by what
   an encounter costs, not by steps.
5. **Optional trainers**: Victory Road, Saffron gym, Hideout stairs side, Mansion (switch modelled), Silph 1F-11F,
   Surge's gym and the Blaine quiz have no forced fights (rotating trainers aside); Route 3, Route 25, Route 6, Route
   8, Rock Tunnel (+Route 9), Tower 3F-6F, Mt Moon (Grunt1 + Miguel), Nugget Bridge (+ the Rocket), Cerulean, Fuchsia,
   Viridian gym (1 each), Celadon gym (2), Mt Ember (1) do.
6. **Routing**: the order is dependency-forced (`docs/glitchless-run/plan.md` s1-2). Walks that avoid grass at a
   cost (`avoid_grass`) lose to the shortest path; bike in hunts lost; bike for the walk to a site is a
   knob (`detour_bike`).
7. **Catch priorities**: throw while passing only at catch rate >= 35-45; cheap fill sites by seconds per
   species (Power Plant, Route 11, Route 21 North, Seafoam B4F, Victory Road 2F, Safari, Vermilion/Fuchsia
   fishing with the Super Rod); Clefairy, Abra (Game Corner), Mr. Mime (trade) and the other gifts; the
   Safari Zone is expensive under its own rules.
8. **Money is the limit on balls early** (1.7k at Cerulean, 13k at Lavender, ~70k at the Hall of Fame).
