# 2026-11-29: skeptical audit of plan 1043-calm (Mewtwo Calm Mind at Champion-2)

Verdict: **the mechanism is right, the engine has no bug that inflates it, the gain is real inside the model (-124 h at WR+2m, -174 h at WR on the 1042 refit model), and the size rests
entirely on the engine's Champion-2 survival (.55 -> .90 of arrivals). No real runner has used it.** CURRENT stays `1043-calm`.

## 1. Engine vs decomp (all checked, no change needed)
* `EFFECT_CALM_MIND`: `battle_scripts_1.s:2733-2760` raises Sp. Atk and Sp. Def by one stage each, `ppreduce` once, target user. Engine: `TwoStat(3, 4, 1)`, `do_move` returns before the accuracy roll, stages clamped -6..+6
  (`battle.rs:243`, `:2211`). Move data `battle_moves.h:4514`: PP 20, priority 0. Pressure does not apply to a user-targeted move; Mewtwo's PP is not tracked, 2-4 Calm Minds never exhaust 20.
* The queued `calm` is a normal move turn: priority 0, so the foe's faster or +1 move acts first, the order roll and every foe hit land as for any move (trace below). No item turn, no stock, no free action;
  the `Sac(Some(CalmMind))` branch exists but the Champion-2 tactic has `sacs: 0`.
* X Special: `item_effects.h:265` raises Sp. Atk only, as the engine's stage 3. So `calm, calm` = `special, special` plus +2 Sp. Def, minus two bag turns of item cost.
* Damage uses stage ratios on the defender's Sp. Def (`staged(d, d_stage)`), crits ignore the defender's positive stages and the attacker's negative ones (`calc_damage`, same as `CalculateBaseDamage`).
  New test `calm_mind_scales_champion_specials_by_hand`: Gyarados Hydro Pump 73-87 / 49-58 / 38-45 and Tyranitar Crunch 112-132 / 76-90 / 57-68 at Sp. Def stage 0 / +1 / +2, and the crit ignoring +2
  (Hydro Pump 174, Crunch 264), re-derived by hand in integer math from the sim's own stats (trainer natures included: Gyarados SpA 126, Tyranitar SpA 147).
  Alakazam's Shadow Ball is **physical in Gen 3** (`IS_TYPE_PHYSICAL`, `battle.h:475`, Ghost < `TYPE_MYSTERY`): 52-62 at every stage, Calm Mind does nothing against it. Psychic is resisted (about 63 at stage 0).
  Calm Mind therefore only helps against Hydro Pump, Crunch (Dark is special), Overheat, Solar Beam and Alakazam's own Psychic; Heracross's Megahorn, Earthquake, Extreme Speed, Aerial Ace, Iron Tail, Sludge Bomb
  (physical Poison) and Shadow Ball ignore it.
* Engine vs the WR's own Champion-2 (`video-analysis/data/calib/v4-e4r2.md`, Mewtwo IVs 5/14, L73, 241 HP): new test `champion2_hits_against_the_wr_mewtwo_match_the_video_totals`:
  Hydro Pump 76-90, Extreme Speed 51-60, Shadow Ball 56-66. The video's single readings (55, 24, 37) are HP counters caught mid-drain by the 4 s sampling; the totals agree (Shadow Ball 238 -> 176 = 62,
  Hydro Pump 164 -> 55 with one sandstorm tick = 94, Extreme Speed 241 -> 178 with one tick = 48). So enemy damage on the Champion-2 Mewtwo is right to within the sampling.

  Gyarados 73, Arcanine 73, Venusaur 75 with Sitrus; IV 255 = 31). The sim reads these. No Haze, Roar, Whirlwind or stat reset on the team; Alakazam has its own Calm Mind and Reflect (the sim's AI plays them, `cv_def_up`).

  Psychic on Gyarados, Arcanine and Venusaur at +2/+2.
* Win rate with 4 Full Restores, Mewtwo IV 20, 5000 fights: no set-up .28, `special,special` .46, `calm` .58, `calm,calm` .90. The gain over `special,special` is the +2 Sp. Def (Hydro Pump, Crunch, Overheat) and
  the two item turns that are no longer paid; a third Calm Mind adds nothing (HP gate).
* AI: `ai_damage` uses the target's staged Sp. Def like `battle_ai_script_commands.c` (so TRY_TO_FAINT sees the boost), `ai_should_switch` and `ai_wants_item` (Full Restore under 1/4) are ported. Nothing lets the player set up for free.

## 5. Stream / WR evidence
`wr-frame-facts.json`: "R2: Lance 2 two (X Special), **Champion 2 none**". WR Champion 2 (`calib/v4-e4r2.md`): Mewtwo Psychic / Fire Blast / Safeguard / Earthquake, one Full Restore, ended 148/241; no Calm Mind, no X Special,

(transcript 3:38:26 "not a single Calm Mind happens, by the way"). **No real runner uses Calm Mind or any Sp. Def boosting at the Champion, so the gain relies purely on the engine's Champion-2 survival**; the WR instead
shows that a plain fight with one heal is survivable (one sample, a 241 HP Mewtwo).

## 6. Caveats that are not calm-specific
* Mewtwo's PP is untracked (the WR's Psychic was 7/10 at 3:04): every Mewtwo plan is optimistic by the same Elixir-class cost; calm adds no Psychic PP.
* The stream's Alakazam crit (198 on a 258 HP Mewtwo) is above the engine's Shadow Ball crit (124) or resisted Psychic crit (115); it is probably an Alakazam at +1/+2 Sp. Atk (its own Calm Mind) or a mid-drain reading. A crit ignores Calm Mind's +Sp. Def either way.
* Calm Mind relies on the first mon dying in one hit (Heracross) and the human choosing Alakazam as the set-up target: both stated rules a human can follow.

## Decision
No engine bug, so `CURRENT` stays `1043-calm`. Quote the 1044 numbers (182 h WR+2m, 250 h WR on the refit model) rather than 1043's. If a Champion-2 survival for the calm line can be measured in the emulator or from a runner, refit
`champion-2` `fatal` first; until then the +-.40 break-even above is the honest error bar.
