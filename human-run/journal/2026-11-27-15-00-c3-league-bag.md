  HP heal and `heal_pick(.., full=true)` put the Full Restore first. Round 2 subtracts `CHAMPION2_RESERVE` from the
  Full Restores only. `FRLG_C1_OLD=1` restores the old behaviour.

  Restore only when none is left; a status: Full Restore (reserve kept as before).

  4F stones keep back the X items' and the Hyper Potions' cost, the 5F X items keep back the Hyper Potions' cost.
  Saffron buys the League's 4 Hyper Potions plus silph-walk's 4 (`SaffronCity_Mart/scripts.inc:26`; Celadon 2F has
  none). The Great Balls moved out of Celadon (the full2 run spent 5400 of 10 757 there) to a new stop after Sabrina
  (plan 1413 `marts` sabrina-badge: Great Balls to 25, 3 Hyper Potions; Saffron sells both, `scripts.inc:25-26`).
* **Indigo** was already as the plan says (`legs2::indigo_mart`, both rounds via `legs3`): the heal count (Hyper +
  Max + Full Restore) is topped up to 14 with Full Restores, as far as the money goes. The count includes Hyper
