## Finding 3: why the sim's carry was 4-5 levels short

**The exp formula is right.** `Cmd_getexp` (`decompiled/src/battle_script_commands.c:3113-3250`: `expYield * level / 7`,
split over the participants, x1.5 trainer battle, x1.5 Lucky Egg, x1.5 traded) is `frlg_mon::exp_gain` as the sim calls it
per KO; nobody holds an Exp. Share or Lucky Egg, the carry fights alone, evolutions are level-driven. Fight for fight the sim's
gains equal the played run's exactly: Giovanni 1 +2357 / +2357, Misty +1340 / +1339, Cerulean rival +1089 / +1089, nugget
bridge +2609 / +2609.

**The cause is the fight count.** The sim fights the sight-cone minimum (`forced-audit.md`: a perfect dodger), the played
run and the runner fight far more. `frlg-human play-battles` (new) replays the canonical s11 `run.ilog` and lists every
battle with `gTrainerBattleOpponent_A` and the lead's exp: 36 trainers the plan does not fight before Koga (Route 3 x3, Mt Moon
1F x3, Luis, Route 25 x5, the Cerulean grunt, Isabelle, Elijah, Route 9/10 + Rock Tunnel x7 more, Aidan, Game Corner and
hideout grunts x4 more, two B1F grunts, Surge's gym x2, channelers x5 more, Lao, Edgar). The controller's walker prices a
sight tile at only 3 steps (`play/walk.rs` `SIGHT`), so it walks into cones. Cumulative exp before the fight (sim 1211 mean /
s11): Giovanni 1 31 399 (L32) / 42 528 (L36); Koga 45 859 (L37) / 71 132 (L42). The gap builds on Route 3 (~700), Cerulean gym
(Luis, 614), Route 25 (~1960), Route 5/6 (~1250), Route 9/10 / Rock Tunnel (~4600), Route 8 (Aidan 1750).

The runner fights them too: the WR overlay's levels (Misty 21, Bike 27, Koga 40 at the fight / 41 at the split, Blaine 42,
Sabrina 46, Giovanni 3 49) and the 2026-11-14 stream run (all of Nugget Bridge / Route 25, L22 -> 26; L36 at Giovanni 1 at WR
pace, `stream-2026-11-14-main97.md`). The sim was 2-3 levels below the WR at every split.

Also found: **hideout Giovanni was fought twice** (`maps_group` over RocketHideout B1F-B4F takes Giovanni's B4F party as a
"grunt"; with `group_n` 3 he is the second of the three hardest). The fight lists replace that group.

## The model change (all default off)

* `fight_lists` (`params.rs`, `route.rs::apply_fight_lists`): per segment the trainer ids fought; replaces a Parties/Group
  segment or inserts `<seg>+fights`. Test `tests/fight_lists.rs`.
* Plan 1251's **runner-level list**: Luis; Route 25 x7; Isabelle; Route 9 / Rock Tunnel / Route 10 x16; Aidan; hideout
  grunts 7/13/18/17/16. Chosen from the s11 list to match the runner's evidence: the sim's level now equals the WR's at
  every split that shows one (Misty 21/21, Bike 27.2/27, Koga 41/41, Blaine 42/42, Sabrina 47/46, Giovanni 3 49/49) and the
  stream's L36 at Giovanni 1. The full s11 list overshoots (Koga 43, Blaine 44, Giovanni 3 51) and is 10 min slower.
* **Windows refit** (`fit_lane.py`, R 1M, seed 1, WR+160m, plan 1252): rock-tunnel..snorlax **2.565 -> 1.038**, ..koga-badge
  **1.35 -> 0.99**, ..blaine/sevii 0.773 -> 0.674, ..giovanni-badge 1.572 -> 1.06, ..indigo-heal 2.314 -> 2.169. The first
  window (1.111) is not identifiable here (model survival saturates at .646 even at power 4.5: those deaths are not lane
  losses) and is kept. Check: every window inside the runner's 95% CI, survival starter -> door .0550 vs runner .0563
  (1132: .0675). So the two windows the audit flagged were indeed missing exp, not skill; the post-Koga ones moved too.
* **`trainer_fit` 0.81 -> 0.6** (plan 1253). With the fights in, the old per-fight time put the sim 8 min behind the WR at
  Giovanni 1 and 12 min at the door (`splits`), while the runner fought the same trainers at WR pace. 0.6 brings the
  no-reset pre-door splits to the WR's (Misty -95 s, Rock Tunnel +13, Giovanni 1 +76, Koga -50, Blaine +47, E4R1 +31). It
  is a fit of the per-fight overhead to splits, not a measurement; one-shot route trainers are cheaper than the 8 gym/rival
  fights the 0.71-0.81 came from. The windows were refit at 0.81 (survival at the lenient rung barely depends on time).

## Finding 4: Koga's Muk

The 35% "engine at s11 stats" of the stand-in audit was a **harness mismatch**: `frlg-human battle` defaults to
`--setup-min 1 --heal-below 0.4`, the plan runs `setup_min_turns` 2 and `heal_below` .6. At setup-min 1 the engine spends the
X Specials on the first Koffing turn 1 (650 of them in 400 fights); the controller (and the sim, which reads the plan) attacks
Koffing and sets up on Muk. **At the plan's policy the engine wins 60.5%** (n 10 000). The emulator, 100 seeds now (40 + 60,
the controller logs `foe_last_move` and both sides' stat stages per turn): **73/100** [64, 81]. Losses: emulator Muk 22,
Weezing 5; engine Muk 27%, second Koffing 6.7%, Weezing 6%.

Checked against the decomp and found equal: `Cmd_accuracycheck` (hit rate per Water Pulse by Muk's evasion stage .495 vs
.533 at the same mean stage +3.0/+3.1), Minimize +1 (`EFFECT_MINIMIZE` -> `AI_CV_EvasionUp`, ported line by line),
Acid Armor (`AI_CV_DefenseUp` via `EFFECT_DEFENSE_UP_2`), Water Pulse and Sludge damage rolls, the Toxic counter (n/16),
Muk's move shares at full HP (Minimize .65 / Acid Armor .2 / Sludge .07 in both). Remaining difference: the engine's Muk uses
Toxic about twice as often (5.6% vs 2.9% of its turns), the carry is poisoned on 31% of Muk turns vs 16%, and dies more
often to toxic ticks at the next Koffing. Not located (`AI_CV_Toxic`, `AI_CBM_Poison` read and match); open.

One real engine bug on the way: the trainer item count treated **`ITEM_FULL_HEAL` as a full HP heal**
(`data.rs`); the AI uses it only as a cure (`AI_ITEM_CURE_CONDITION`, `battle_ai_switch_items.c:606-638`; Hyper Potion is
`AI_ITEM_HEAL_HP` at < 1/4, `:599-605`). Misty, Surge, Erika, Koga, Sabrina and Blaine carry one. MODEL CHANGE
`ai_cure_items` (default off; the controller's predictions use the honest count), test `a_full_heal_is_not_an_hp_heal`.
Effect nil (1256: -0.04 h): Koga's mons rarely sit below 1/4 with a heal left.

**Koga window re-evaluated**: at honest levels the fit gives 0.99 (was 1.35), i.e. no Koga stand-in is needed once the carry
is L40-41 there; the residual engine-vs-emulator gap (60 vs 73% at s11 stats) errs pessimistic and is absorbed by the fit.
