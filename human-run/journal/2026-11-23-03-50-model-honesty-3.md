## The bugs

1. **Level evolutions of mons that never fight** (`level_evo_honest`, MODEL). The model evolved a `via: "level"`
   pre-evolution for 2000 frames whenever its catch level was within `level_gap` 8 of the evolution level. In the game
   a boxed or benched mon gains no exp: `Cmd_getexp` pays the sent-in party mons and Exp. Share holders only
   (`decompiled/src/battle_script_commands.c:3148-3175`); the evolution check runs on a level-up
   (`decompiled/src/battle_main.c:3896`; Rare Candy `decompiled/src/party_menu.c:5169`) with `param <= level`
   (`decompiled/src/pokemon.c:5076-5078`). Correction to the brief: FRLG does have an Exp. Share (Route 15 gate aide,
   `decompiled/data/maps/Route15_WestEntrance_2F/scripts.inc:26-38`, needs a caught count), but it halves the exp of
   the holder's party and goes on one mon, so it is no route for ~10 evolutions. Honest version: Rare Candies,
   `max(1, level - catch level)` per evolution out of the same stock the carry eats at Indigo, priced as a candy
   evolution. On 1316 the model had 6.5 species from `level` at Indigo (Pidgeot, Raticate, Fearow, Sandslash,
   Nidorino, Parasect, Venomoth, Dugtrio, Persian, Machoke, Slowbro, Magneton, Dodrio, Weezing); honest, the candy
   evolutions rise 0.7 -> 3.3, catches 36.5 -> 39.8 (wild Raticate / Fearow / Sandslash / Machoke / Persian / Slowbro
   replace the evolutions; Dodrio, Weezing and most Dugtrio are gone), and **the carry reaches E4 round 1 at Lv 52.4
   instead of 55.3** (the WR's level there is ~51, the League refit's "WR level", journal 2026-11-22-12-00): the old
   model's Lv 55 carry was bought with candies that also paid its free evolutions. No-reset clock at E4R1 start
   9975 s vs 9579 s (+396 s: catching the evolved forms and the extra dex fill). Plan response: cap 1 candy per
   evolution (1601/1612, -395 / -735 h); dropping all level evolutions is far worse (+4160 h: the dex fill).
   Left as is: the Kakuna/Beedrill and Metapod/Butterfree `specials` (`gift` requiring Weedle / Caterpie). The WR
   levels the bugs by switch-in exp inside the Silph fights (stream 11-14, `video-analysis/stream-2026-11-14-main97.md`
   lines 128-129; WR VOD 2:05-2:07), cheap at Lv 3-10 (Medium Fast `n^3`: 1000 exp to Lv 10); the half-exp cost to
   the carry is not modelled (small).

2. **Safari East "duplicates" West: not a model bug.** LG tables (`decompiled/src/data/wild_encounters.json`): East
   = Nidoran F/M, Doduo, Exeggcute, Paras, Nidorina, Parasect, Kangaskhan, Pinsir; West = the same minus Paras /
   Parasect / Pinsir plus Venonat (15%), Venomoth, Tauros. The controller ran West first, after which East adds only
   Paras/Parasect/Pinsir; the plan's order is East first (sim: East +3.5 species in 154 s, West +1.3 in 87 s with
   `safari_stop` 5). Geography: Center -> West has no walkable warp; `bin/frlg-mapgraph` routes Center (26,29) ->
   West (28,15) as 195 tiles via East and North, and the `safari-walk` 460 tiles (Gold Teeth `SafariZone_West/map.json`
   (28,14), the East Leaf Stone (36,17) `SafariZone_East/map.json`) already pass through East, so `on_route` with no
   detour is honest. West-only (1604) +260 h: refuted. **Controller instruction: hunt East first (plan order).**
   With bug 1 fixed East matters more (its Parasect replaces the Paras level evolution).

3. **Koga heal: the model had none, and the risk lane got a free one** (`risk_entry_paid`, MODEL). `center-heal@koga-badge`
   is skipped in the Torrent phase, so the model never visits a Fuchsia Center. In the risk lane, Koga always started
   at full HP (411/411 traced, `FRLG_RISK_TRACE`): the timing lane's pre-fight entry heal (free potions, Torrent window
   floor = Koga's best hit x `torrent_hits`, usually above max HP) set `hp_frac` to 1.0, and the lane sync's `>= .999`
   shortcut copied it to the lane, so the comment "the risk lane does not inherit them" was false whenever the
   free potions filled the bar (also Blaine 341/341). Honest: the lane pays its own entry potions (the paid `risk_potions`
   before `koga-badge`, the 1413 bag after): Koga's lane spends ~0.9 potion more (5.5 -> 4.6 left), risk death 13.6% ->
   13.1% (n 411/373, n.s.), and money falls everywhere in the Torrent phase: +234 h. The 1413 potion bag does not cover
   Koga (it starts at `koga-badge`); PP is not short at Koga in the model (slots 9-13 PP traced; the controller's low PP
   came from its extra fights). Plan responses: a Fuchsia Center heal before Koga (`torrent_heals: ["safari-walk"]`)
   +54 n.s. (not worth it in the model); the controller's heal after the badge +2170 h (it lifts the carry out of the
   Torrent window for Blaine / Sevii): **refuted, the controller should not heal after Koga**.

4. **League round 2.** Checked against the played branches and the WR:
   - AI heals: the sim gives the rematch Champion its 4 Full Restores (`decompiled/src/data/trainers.h:7395-7403`)

     Full Restore in the engine (`battle.rs` `Action::FullRestore` cures status and confusion); a Full Restore costs

   - The sim's per-try win (`fightdist`, WR+160m, 1316): Lance-2 0.948, Champion-2 0.902; with `mewtwo_power` 1.0
     (the controller's engine-pure Mewtwo): 0.906 / 0.773 (SENS 1610: +832 h). Played: Champion-2 2 wins in 9 branches
     (pg-canon5 1/5, v3 1/4), Lance-2 2 deaths in 4 (all in pg-canon5, at ¥30k money). The playing controller uses

     Blastoise follow-up would make the sim more optimistic, not less, so it is not the cause of the gap and was not
     modelled.

     Champion-2 death rate of the played runs is bot state (weaker Mewtwo, fewer heals), not a model bug; the
     unidentified part is `mewtwo_power`, already a fitted stand-in; do not refit it to the bot's deaths.**
