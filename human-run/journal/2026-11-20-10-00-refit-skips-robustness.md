## 3. Conditional skip lever (new engine mechanism)
`Params::cond_skips = [{segs, behind}]` (`params.rs` `CondSkip`; `sim.rs` attempt loop; `wr_at` per segment from `wrsplits::WR_SPLITS`): a listed segment is not played when the attempt's clock at the last WR split passed is more than `behind` seconds past the WR's time there. Skipped means no frames, no catches, no items; later fill hunts (the plan's `fill` list before the League) recover missing species, and the dex-conditioned reset rule sees the lower count. Test `cond_skips_skip_only_when_behind_the_wr`; `report` prints "skipped ..." rows.
What is skippable (all from the plan's own segments; the dex count then costs the later fill, `Kind::OakCheck`): Power Plant (fly-lavender-2, powerplant-walk, powerplant-out, Thunder Stone pickup, hunt: 18.6 -> 21.7 species, `report` dex column), Safari hunts (12.5 -> 17.1), Diglett's Cave hunt + Mr. Mime trade, Route 22/23 hunts, Bond Bridge hunt. **Not skippable**: the Safari trip itself (Gold Teeth/HM04 at `safari-fee-and-hms`) and the gyms.
`skipknobs.py` (`descend.py --set skips`): 9 levers x thresholds -420..+240 s behind (the model mean sits 2-7 min *ahead* of the WR splits). At WR+2m, R 800k, seed 1 with seed-2 confirm: **0 of 54 accepted**; negative thresholds (skip unless well ahead) cost +1.5 to +325 h; thresholds >= +60 s almost never fire (the pace resets at the splits fire first, diff 0.0). Two seed-1 accepts (powerplant behind>60: -0.03; safari-center behind>-60: -0.65 [-1.26,-0.11]) failed confirmation (-0.02 [-0.35, .28]; +0.22 [-0.22, .75]). Conclusion: the lever is free in the engine but worth nothing in the model: a skipped hunt's species are bought back by the fill at about the same price, and a run late enough to want the skip is already reset by the aggr gates. Not adopted; the mechanism stays (default empty).
