Round 1 needs about 13 heals before the Champion with the carry as it is (2.1-3.6 + 2.6-3.4 + 2.5-3.0 + 5.6-6.0); the
bag holds 9 (model: 8-11 too, `FRLG_SIM_TRACE` `LEAGUE topup`: purse 14-20k, 4-6 bought). The controller's
rooms spend the heals the engine says they need. I found no room where the played turns differ from the engine's:

* **Agatha, Gengar 1, turns 2-5 "Surf does nothing"**: Double Team (`foe_last_move DOUBLE_TEAM`, `foe_stages[7]` 7, 8, 9).
  Evasion +n scales the 100 % moves by 75 / 60 / 50 / 43 % (`gAccuracyStageRatios`, `battle_script_commands.c`
  `AccuracyCheck`), so three or four misses are 5 % events, not a controller error. The policy reads the stage (the
  controller fills `Mon::stages[5..6]` from `gBattleMons`, `play/battle.rs:185`) and the sim trace shows the same sequences
  (`FRLG_BATTLE_TRACE`: Double Team to +5 / +6 on 4 of 30 fights). Nothing in Blastoise's moves ignores evasion.
* **Haunter (Hypnosis, Dream Eater, Curse, Mean Look)** and Gengar 2 (Hypnosis, Nightmare): sleep is cured with a Full Restore
  (`status_bad`), as in the sim (`sim` trace: `FullRestore` at `Sleep(2..4)`). Dream Eater never fired in 30 sim fights and
  none of the played ones.
* **Switching**: never chosen in the League by either (sacs 0, `fight_tactics`), the dead branches after Blastoise falls send
  Lapras / Bellsprout / Weedle / Caterpie / Pidgey, which lose in 5 turns.
* **X items**: used as planned (Lorelei 3 Special, Bruno 2 Special + Speed, Agatha 1 Special, Lance Accuracy / 2 Special; `calm`
  is skipped, Blastoise has no Calm Mind). 8-9 turns a run, none wasted on a dead fight except Lance's 3 X items at 0 heals.
* **Heals are not rationed across rooms**: `heal_stock` is the bag, the policy spends it at its HP thresholds. The sim does the
  same (one shared pool), so that is the model's design, not a controller difference; a human would hold back for Lance, and
  then lose Bruno / Agatha (the 4-heal Agatha is 44 %).

Cheaper heals do not help: with the sim, 5 heals as 2 FR + 3 Max Potion win Agatha 28 % against 49 % for 5 FR (FR carries the
status cure), 3 Full Heals added to 3 FR give +2.7 points for 1800 yen, one more FR +9.6 for 3000. The plan's Full Restores
stand (`indigo_mart` event "League heals bought as Full Restores").

## Model bug (not fixed here: shared policy / plan)

1. **`league_power` 1.2 buys the model about 1.5 heals per room.** The same fight with the real carry (`--power 1.0`) costs 3.6 /
   3.4 / 3.0 / 6.0 heals against 2.1 / 2.6 / 2.5 / 5.6, and Bruno's win rate goes 43 % -> 24 % with the bag unlimited. The controller
   has no such power (level 52-53, no held item; the model's carry is Lv 51 plus the 1.2). Round 1 as played needs ~16 heals
   by the Champion, the model prices 9-11. Evidence: `c5-simheals.py` output above, `c5-b3x.log` (Bruno 9 of 22).
2. **No whiteout cost beyond the walk**: the `fatal` table (lorelei 0, bruno 0.17, agatha 0, lance 0.097) is far from the played
   Agatha (7 of 82), Lance (1 of 95) rate with the bag the model itself buys.
3. `danger` is a no-crit number (`Seen::danger`): Sky Uppercut did 126 (danger 70), Shadow Punch 70 (danger 37), so the
   heal-at-low-HP margin is one crit short. Same in the model's `danger`.

## Fixes (controller)

1. `7c0df762` **The controller now counts the trainer's Full Restores it has seen** (`play/battle.rs` `Ctl::ai_used`,
   `Fs::set_ai_heals`). The sim's own fight decrements `fs.ai_heals` in `act_enemy`; the controller never did, so the
   policy kept expecting a Full Restore at every foe under 1/4 HP (`ai_wants_item`, `battle_ai_switch_items.c:562-598`)
   after the real ones were gone (Dewgong, Hitmonchan, Gengar healed in the played turns). `FRLG_C5_OLD=1` restores the old
   behaviour. **Effect on the three replay seeds: none** (a31..a33 reproduce b31..b33 action for action: 82 Agatha and 95 Lance
   fights with the same turns and heals); no state in these replays reaches a decision that reads the count. Kept as a
   correctness fix, not as a result.
2. `68b462d3` **Indigo mart sells the unused badge TMs and the Super Potion** (`legs2::indigo_mart`): TM06 Toxic, TM19 Giga
   Drain, TM28 Dig, TM39 Rock Tomb (no plan `tms` move; Mewtwo's Shock Wave / Earthquake / Calm Mind TMs stay) sell for half
   price (`tm_case.c:1295`) = 5500 yen, plus 350 for the Super Potion: 9 Full Restores instead of 7-8 (purse 29 855 against
   24 005, ¥2855 left; `c5-u31`). It exposed a bug in `shop::sell_from_tm_case`: the loop pressed A until the TM Case list was back
   and that A sold the **next** TM (in `c5-t31` a sale of TM28 turned over TM34 and TM38 too, +5250), fixed there
   (`tm_case.c:1289-1316`: the item leaves the bag in `Task_DoSaleOfTMs`, the A / B after it only closes the message).
