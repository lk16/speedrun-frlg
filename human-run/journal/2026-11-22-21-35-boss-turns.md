# 2026-11-22: boss turns and the level-up double count (fights at the WR's price, done honestly)

Branch `human-run-play`, worktree agent. Plans 1420-1426, rows in `scores.jsonl`. Parent: model of record
`1285-wr-fight-speed` (`trainer_cost` [791, 547, 211] = x0.944 of the played-run fit). The predecessor (journal
`2026-11-22-21-00-wr-fight-timing`) found two errors that cancel: (a) the sim takes 202 decisions on the WR's 67
VOD-timed fights where the WR took 237, all of the gap in rivals / leaders; (b) level-up text is inside the fitted
`trainer_cost` (0.43 level-ups per played episode) and charged again via `level_up`.

## (a) Why the sim's boss fights are short: `carry_power` 2.0

- `FRLG_SIM_TRACE` on 1285: the timing lane one-shots every rival and leader mon. Cerulean rival L21: 4 actions for
  4 mons (the WR: 7; the played bot: 8); S.S. Anne 4 for 4 (WR 8); Sabrina 4 for 4 with 3 X items queued and none
  used (`setup_min_turns` 2 never triggers when the foe falls in one hit). The carry's Sp. Atk is printed at 2x
  (Wartortle L21: 82; a 30-IV Rash Wartortle has ~41). `carry_power` 2.0 (params.rs doc) was fitted long ago on 16 WR
  fights, when the engine needed 1.5-4x the WR's actions; since then the sim got the WR's levels, candies, fight
  list, X items, Persim etc., and 2.0 now overshoots. X items / switches are not the cause: X-item turns are already
  counted as actions (`battle.rs` `out.turns` per loop iteration, `item_turns`), the sim simply never needs them.
- The engine at `carry_power` 1.0 overshoots instead (309 actions on the list; Rock Tunnel 45-53 vs 30), and the
  timing lane's losses rise (it is also a death source next to the risk lane, whose windows were fitted with the
  timing lane at 2.0). So `carry_power` itself is not refit here.
- **MODEL CHANGE `turn_power` / `turn_power_boss`** (`params.rs`, `sim.rs` `sim_battle`): the clock pays the
  actions of a copy of each non-League trainer fight with the carry's Atk / Sp. Atk at `turn_power` (route) or
  `turn_power_boss` (rivals, leaders, Giovanni: `params::is_boss`) instead of `carry_power` x the fight's `power`;
  same moves, items, HP and game-RNG state. Outcome, HP, items and exp stay with the fight at `carry_power`, so
  survival is unchanged. A FIT on the 67 fights (fightdist, `--from rival-lab`, R 8000, per-segment means over
  attempts that completed the segment; `video-analysis/tools/fight-timing/simdecisions.py`):

  One factor cannot fit both groups (route trainers need ~1.4, bosses ~1.0 = the audited engine). WR / sim actions,

  policy-bound, the copy's power barely moves them), Sabrina (5.7 vs 7) and the Route 22 rival (10.5 vs 13).

## (b) The level-up double count

- `refit.py` (predecessor) on the 83 played episodes: free fit a/t/m/level-up [746, 529, 243, 459]; with the
  level-up fixed at the sim's 240 f: **[798, 536, 251]** (CIs [650, 976], [484, 599], [160, 329]).
- The WR factor recomputed for it: WR episodes (3684 s) minus their level-up text (36.4 level-ups on the list, the
  sim's count, x 240 f = 146 s; the carry has the WR's level at every split) over the new cost at the WR's own
  decisions: **0.938 [0.904, 0.981]** (old: 0.944). `trainer_cost` = 0.938 x [798, 536, 251] = **[749, 503, 235]**.
- `Probe::fights` now carries the carry's level-ups per fight (5th field; `fightdist` prints it).

## (c, part 1) A third double count: `tcost_whole`

With (a) + (b) the core price on the WR list (a + t x actions + m x mons + level-ups) is 3693 s vs the WR's 3684 s,
but the sim's fight clock is 3900 s. `FRLG_DBG_EXTRA` splits the rest: 84 s pre-fight bag trips (Elixirs before
Silph / Route 22 with the PP nearly gone, pre-heals), 126 s in battle: input jitter (~23 s), event reactions
(~11 s) and the risk lane's paid in-battle potions (`risk_pay`, one `turn()` each, ~90 s); no sacrifices on this
list. The WR-fitted price already holds the WR's reactions and mashing, and the copy's actions already hold its heal
turns, so those 126 s are counted twice. **MODEL CHANGE `tcost_whole`**: a priced trainer fight (with
`trainer_cost` and `turn_power`) is the whole battle; the jitter / reaction draws are kept (common random numbers)
but not charged, and the lane's in-battle potions cost money only. Pre-fight bag trips stay. Sim fight clock on the
list then 3814 s (core 3693 + bag trips + retries after whiteouts).

## Verdict

- **Recommended: 1423-boss-turns-whole as model of record** (MODEL CHANGE, +179 h [138, 234] vs 1285). It fixes
  both errors the predecessor named and a third double count (jitter / reactions / risk-lane potion turns on top of a
  WR-fitted price), with the fight price checked component by component against the WR: actions 239.7 vs 237
  (bosses 0.98 [0.88, 1.08] of the WR), core seconds 3693 vs 3684, no-reset clock at Giovanni 3 -43 s (1285: -151 s,
  from two cancelling errors). The `turn_power` pair is a FIT on 67 fights (one more FIT next to `carry_power`), not a
  mechanism; the honest mechanism (why the audited engine at 1.0 overshoots route trainers by ~40 %) is open.
- If `tcost_whole` is judged too aggressive, 1422 (864 h) is the same fix with the extras charged.
- The League agent's refit was to be combined with 1285: it should be recombined with 1423 (no League keys touched).
