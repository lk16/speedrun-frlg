# 2026-11-22: segment-cost calibration of the played run (controller vs model vs WR)

Branch `human-run-play`, worktree agent. Tables and constant-by-constant verdicts are in `calibration.md`
("Segment-cost calibration from the played run"). This entry is the method, the model changes and the
**ranked list of controller inefficiencies** for the controller agent (`crates/frlg-human/src/play/` was not
touched here).

## Method

- `crates/frlg-human/examples/cal_trace.rs`: replays a run's `run.ilog` from a checkpoint savestate (booted with
  the BIOS like `Recorder::from_reset`; on the HLE core the replay diverges) to the next checkpoint and writes
  one CSV row per frame: `gMain.callback2`, `inBattle`, `gBattleTypeFlags`, map and tile, `gPlayerAvatar`
  flags/runningState/tileTransitionState/preventStep, `sLockFieldControls`, the script context, the message box,
  `gPaletteFade.active`, and `sWildEncounterData`'s rate buff and step counter. All 81 stage ends (New Game ->
  Indigo mart, `1122-lgdescent-s11` + `fwd-main`) reproduce the RAM hashes in `checkpoints.jsonl`.
- `human-run/tools/cal_decompose.py` (classes), `cal_battles.py` (per battle + fit), `cal_windows.py` (per WR
  window). Scratch CSVs are in the session scratchpad (`cal-t-*`, `cal-p-*`, `cal-f-*`), not committed.
- `crates/frlg-human/examples/cal_encprobe.rs`: paces or tap-turns on grass from a savestate and prints the
  encounter rolls.

## Findings

1. **Without its overhead the controller matches the model** from Giovanni 1 to Koga: 2006 s played, 501 s of
   overhead (373 s of it avoidable trainers), 1505 s vs the model's 1522 s. The 1342 s vs 891 s "Giovanni 1 ->
   Snorlax" gap in the brief is mostly overhead (340 s), the played Surge-first order (the WR folds Surge into
   the flute leg), and the Lavender heal.
2. **Battles are priced right per turn**: 52 bridge-driven trainer battles sum to 0.95x / 1.02x the `calib_wr`
   formula at the played turns and party sizes. Before Giovanni 1 (older stand-in driver) the windows are slow
   because of the number of battles and turns (Misty window: 14 battles, 57 turns, 846 s vs the model's 429 s of
   fights).
3. **Model too optimistic** (changed): the new-species catch tail (dex page, `battle_scripts_2.s:73-91`), ball
   misses, indoor walking speed (gyms and the tower forbid bike and running), Fly.
4. **Model too pessimistic** (changed): wild Runs at high level (45 Runs at 635-657 f field to field vs ~832
   modelled) and Safari RUN.
5. **Not changed, open**: doors and stairs (115-160 / 59 f each; ~90 s from Giovanni 1 to Koga) are in no walk
   segment; Poke/Great Ball misses through the bag are 690-880 f (the 555 is from Safari balls); TM/HM teaching
   through the TM case 870 f.
6. **Encounter rolls**: the game rolls at every `T_TILE_CENTER`, turns included
   (`decompiled/src/field_control_avatar.c:138-141`: `checkStandardWildEncounter` does not need `MOVING`; the
   Safari step counter is under `tookStep`, which does). Pacing a reversal costs 16 frames per tile for two
   rolls; **tap-turning in place rolls once per ~10 frames and spends no Safari steps** (probe: 6 trials, battle
   after 7-8 turns from a fresh cooldown). The model's 8.2 frames per hunt "step" is right per roll.
   Plan idea (not scored): Safari hunts by spinning, so `safari_steps` never binds.

## Controller inefficiencies, ranked (for the controller agent)

Seconds are what the played seed-11 run lost against the same run without the fault. "Per visit" items recur at
every Safari / hunt.

1. **Avoidable trainers: ~373 s Giovanni 1 -> Koga** (audited with `human-run/tools/forced.py`): Pokemon Tower
   Patricia, Laurel, Jody, Paula, Angelica (172 s; forced are 5F Karina-or-Tammy and 6F Emilia + Jennifer only),
   Vermilion Gym Baily + Dwayne during the can search (90 s; only Tucker forced), Fuchsia Gym Edgar (70 s; Kirk
   forced), Cycling Road Biker Lao (40 s; forced.py finds no forced fight on Route 17, slope not modelled).
   After Koga not audited; the Erika window (266 s of trainers vs the WR's whole 176 s window) and the Mansion /
   Cinnabar trainers (358 s in the Blaine window vs the model's 84 s of fights) look like the same fault. Fix:
   plan walks with the sight cones as walls (the planner has a cone tie-breaker, not a constraint).
2. **Hunt budget read as tiles, not encounter rolls: ~120-170 s per Safari visit.** The plan's hunt `steps` are
   the sim's rolls (8.2 f each, one per step). Pacing back and forth rolls twice per tile (turn + step), so the
   East hunt's 90 tiles were 180 rolls and 20 encounters (20416 f, 342 s; the model's mean for the hunt is 154 s).
   Fix: pace `steps/2` tiles, or better tap-turn in place (`steps` turns, ~10 f each, no Safari steps).
3. **Hunting with no balls: ~166 s** (Bond Bridge hunt, frames 534k-544k: 9 encounters, 4 weakened then "no ball
   in the bag", fled; 9963 frames, nothing caught). Check the ball count before a hunt and buy or skip it.
4. **Safari row bumps: ~55 s per Safari pass** (2784 bump frames + turns in the canonical pass, the same rows in
   `dex-safari1`): East y=8 (x 23-33), West y=11/12 and y=19/20, North y=20. Each attempt holds the direction
   ~63 frames before re-planning one tile over. Probably one-way ledges or a collision the planner reads as
   walkable; mark the tile blocked after the first bump instead of trying its neighbour.
5. **Transport: ~113 s Giovanni 1 -> Indigo** of walking/running where the bike was legal (39 s to Koga, 74 s
   after; e.g. to-vermilion ran 274 tiles with the Bicycle unregistered, Fuchsia streets run, Sevii routes run).
   Register the bike right after it is obtained and mount on every map with `allow_cycling` and not indoor;
   the Safari Zone allows the bike too (`SafariZone_*/map.json`).
6. **Idle / turn / bump on the field: ~210 s Giovanni 1 -> Indigo** (90 s to Koga, 121 s after; Victory Road
   alone 34 s with 89 bumps, 1403 idle frames). Mostly planning pauses after battles and turn-in-place before
   steps.
7. **Ball throws through the bag: ~3 s per miss** (Great Ball misses 687-883 f vs 509-690 for Safari balls):
   the bag pocket/cursor is re-navigated each throw.
8. **Two extra Koga turns and the Fuchsia heal: ~45 s** (the heal is a model bug already queued: NEXT item 2).
9. **Event log only: every Cut is logged twice** (~285 frames apart; the trace shows one Cut). No time lost;
   fix the duplicate `cut a tree` event.

Not controller faults (real costs the model needs): the Lavender heal, the long paralysed Surge fight (RNG), the
trash cans, doors and stairs.
