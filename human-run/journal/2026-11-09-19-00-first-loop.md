# 2026-11-09: simulator built, plan 001 to 011

## What exists
- `crates/frlg-human` (`frlg-human ladder|eval|compare|report|dist|starter`), data from
  `human-run/tools/gen_dex.py` into `human-run/data/dex.json`, loop helpers `try.py`,
  `variant.py`, `promote.py`. Every evaluation, accepted or refuted, is a line in `scores.jsonl`.
- Gating derived from the decomp (cited in `plans/001-crude.md`): National Dex needs Kanto caught >= 60
  at Oak after the first Hall of Fame, One Island visited (free via Blaine's Bill scene); rematches
  need `FLAG_SYS_CAN_LINK_WITH_RS` from Celio (Ruby from Mt Ember, Sapphire from the Five Island
  warehouse). Rematch parties are level 63-75.

## Result
Current best `011-amber`: rung **WR+10m**, EHT ~4000 h at R=160000 (100 finishes). Model no-reset
finish time p10 3:46, mean 3:56 against WR 3:25. Jitter N=0 gives WR+5m, N=8 gives WR+10m.
**This is a score of the model, not of a human.** 87% of modelled time is `Guess` constants
(battle turn costs, trainer overheads, gym interiors, all post-game text). Nothing is calibrated on
libmgba beyond the pre-Brock ledger text. The model is probably pessimistic overall: it is far slower
than the WR, so the sanity alarm (too fast) did not fire, but absolute rungs mean little.

## What was slow
- Oak's fill hunts (getting from ~34 to 60 species) were 1 h+; the dex is the whole game here.
- In-route hunts cost 100-500 s per species: removing them was the biggest single win.
- Whiteouts at Champion/Rival fights when the carry is under-levelled (team exp is not modelled).

## Refuted
- Pre-E4 grind (any size), Mewtwo (Master Ball), starter min total 70, extra Leaf/Water stones,
  `fight_until_level` 20-40, no bicycle, `reset_aggr` 0.3/0.8/1.0 (see scores.jsonl; aggressive
  resets use the *mean* remaining time and kill the lucky tail, so the rung loosens).

## Caveats found
- Rung-first comparison plus EHT at tight rungs is extremely noisy (finishes ~50-100); CIs are
  bootstrap over blocks of 100 attempts.
- The ladder cap is 1M attempts; rungs depend on it.
