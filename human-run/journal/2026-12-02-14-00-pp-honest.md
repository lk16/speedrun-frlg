## 1. PP model verified against decompiled/
Correct as modelled:
* Base PP: Psychic 10, Fire Blast 5, Earthquake 10, Calm Mind 20 (`decompiled/src/data/battle_moves.h` `.pp`). A TM-taught move starts at base PP with no bonus; replacing a move drops its bonus.
* PP is charged once per use at move start, after the paralysis / flinch / sleep checks, before the accuracy roll (`Cmd_ppreduce`, `battle_script_commands.c:1122`): the sim does the same.
* Ether +10 one move, Max Ether full one move, Elixir +10 all, Max Elixir full all (`src/data/pokemon/item_effects.h:116-134`); Centre refills to `CalculatePPWithBonus` (`script_pokemon_util.c:17-40`).
* Max Elixir locations Route 17 `Route17/map.json:266` (14,157) and Route 23 `Route23/map.json:621` (9,8) are both HIDDEN items (face the tile, press A), as is every other Ether / Elixir / PP Up ball here: the frame costs for the detours are guesses.
* Pressure: `Cmd_ppreduce` adds 1 PP when the target has Pressure (single target), counts Pressure foes for spread moves. The sim ignores Pressure, which is harmless: the only Pressure species in League / rematch parties is Lance's Aerodactyl, and trainer Pokemon always get ability slot 0 (personality is `0x78/0x80/0x88 + nameHash << 8`, low bit 0, `battle_main.c:1557-1607`; slot chosen by `personality & 1`, `pokemon.c:1855`), i.e. Rock Head. Mewtwo's PP is never spent on wild Pokemon in the model. No Pressure foe in any R1/R2 League or Champion party (`src/data/trainer_parties.h`).
* Stale / wrong in the idea list: **no mart sells Ether, Elixir, PP Up or Berries**: grep of every `data/**/*.inc` and `map.json` finds them only as item balls and hidden items (`item_ball_scripts.inc`, map.json). "Buy Ethers with money" is impossible; money only buys heals / X items.
* PP Ups: +20% of base per PP Up, max 3 per move (`pokemon.c:3898`, `:4207`); Psychic 10 -> 12/14/16, Fire Blast 5 -> 6/7/8. Sources (hidden): Rocket Hideout B1F (16,17), Route 17 (4,68), Celadon City (55,20), Silph 5F (32,9), Route 13 (23,14), Three Island (5,13); PP Max Two Island Cape Brink (16,28).
Model error found: none in the PP arithmetic. The model lacked the PP Up bonus and Ether kinds (added now, see 3). Pressure ignored (harmless as above). Also: the bag-trip cost (500 f per Elixir) is a guess.
