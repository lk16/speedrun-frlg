## API

```rust
pub fn fight(f: &mut HumanFeed, obs: &Observer, log: &mut EventLog, cfg: &BattleCfg)
    -> Result<BattleReport, RouteError>;
let cfg = BattleCfg::new(&ctx, "misty");                                  // trainer: plan segment name
let cfg = BattleCfg { wild: WildPlan::Catch, ..BattleCfg::new(&ctx, "mt-moon") }; // wild: Flee|Catch|Fight
let rep = fight(&mut feed, &obs, &mut log, &cfg)?;   // rep.end: Won | Lost | Ran | Caught | Other(u8)
```

Call it with the battle on screen (or with the trainer's pre-battle text up: it mashes A until
`in_battle`). It returns when `gMain.callback2` is `CB2_Overworld` again (after any evolution); the
leader's post-battle speech is the route script's. `ctx` is `Plan::ctx()`; `seg` picks the plan's
`fight_tactics` entry and the Torrent phase (`torrent_until`); wild battles use the sim's own
`"wild"` key.

## Model bugs and model-vs-actual found (the valuable part)

1. **Rival SS Anne is a coin flip with real stats.** `outlook()` from the real turn-1 state
   (Wartortle L20ish, 62 HP) says 45% win; 3 of 6 seeds lost (Ivysaur's Sleep Powder, then
   Razor Leaf). The plan's sim rarely dies here because the carry fights at `carry_power` 2.0
   (timing lane) and the risk lane at `risk_power` 2.0 for this fight; the controller feeds the
   policy real stats. The stand-in powers hide a real death risk before `no_salvage_until`.
2. **The sim's fighter never faints.** Losing the lead mid-fight has no policy in the sim (it is a
   loss); the controller sends the highest-level mon left and logs `model-vs-actual`.
   After that the helpers (Paras L6, Weedle L5, Pidgey L3) only feed Ivysaur exp.
3. **Healing while asleep.** The greedy policy heals with a Potion when in KO range even when the
   fighter is asleep (`Status1::Sleep`): the heal buys a turn the fighter cannot use (seed 13 healed
   twice asleep, then fainted). No Awakening/Full Heal in the model outside Full Restore.
4. **Potion tier.** The sim's heal is `heal_amount(level)` = Super Potion (50) from L15; the s11
   bag at SS Anne held only Potions (20). The controller now feeds the real potion's HP into
   `Tactics::heal` and caps `heal_stock` by the bag (the sim leaves it unlimited outside the
   League); a bag with no heal at all is logged (Sammy's fight).
5. **Ball money.** The plan wants Great Balls (`ball: great`), the s11 bag at Mt. Moon held two Poke
   Balls: thrown with `bag_ball`'s fallback, then no ball and a flee (seed 6). The old controller
   logged the same ("out of ball #4 mid-catch").
6. **Weakening is abstract in the sim** (`weaken`: 1-2 turns to `weaken_hp`, `weaken_ko` risk). The
   controller needs a move: `battle::weaken_move` (best expected damage whose max roll, crit
   included, leaves the foe alive). First version without the crit KO'd Paras with Water Gun
   (17-20 of 21 HP). A controller-only rule until the sim models it the same way.
7. **Battle style SHIFT in the s11 save.** Every trainer mon change asks "Will you change?"; the
   mash answers YES and the controller backs out of the party menu with B: a time loss the model
   does not have. A runner sets SET in the options (the opening segment should).
8. **Party RAM can sit unencrypted.** `gPlayerParty[0]` at Misty's first menu held its secure block
   in the clear (checksum matches with key 0); the reader validates the key by checksum
   (`CalculateBoxMonChecksum`). Not a model bug, but any other reader of party moves/species
   (overworld controller) has to do the same.
9. **Confirmed right:** the trainer's send-out order (`most_suitable`) matched the game 5/5 times
   (Rival Cerulean, Rival SS Anne, Miguel); Misty's and the rivals' parties and stats match the
   dex's trainer data; the wild mon's stat generation matches; `replace_slot` learned Bite over
   Withdraw and skipped Rapid Spin like the old controller.

## Known not to work / untested

- Not exercised on real states yet: X items and Full Restores (bag Items pocket path is the same
  as the Potion's, minus the party menu), sacrifice switches (`Action::Sac`), drag-out switch-back,
  the evolution scene and its move prompt (handled by the same summary-screen rule), Safari Zone
  (falls to Run), double battles, the Old Man / first battle controllers, trainers' X items.
- Run from a trainer battle is impossible and never chosen; `Flee` with a failed escape repeats.
- `fs.last_move` is the player's last move, not `gCurrentMove`; screens are read from RAM, weather
  and Leech Seed / Substitute are not.
- Ability comes from the dex species (first ability), not from the PID bit.
- Out of PP: the policy's Struggle stand-in (first slot) would hit a "no PP" message; untested.
- No `.ilog` from `battle-test` (events.jsonl, end.png, end.state only).
